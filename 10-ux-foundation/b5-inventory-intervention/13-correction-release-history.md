# B5 — Correction, Release and Historical Truth

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Case | Preserve | Safe effect | Open boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wrong Unit | Original request/report, actor and source. | Correct/supersede to intended Unit; recompute only effective truth. | Downstream conflict/remediation. |
| Wrong range | Original range and correction evidence. | Amend/replacement with explicit authority. | Partial overlap semantics. |
| Wrong reason | Original reason/source and corrected reason. | Preserve audit; reason does not rewrite history. | Disclosure/retention. |
| Duplicate Block | Both provenance records. | Identify/resolve duplicate without double-counting effective constraint. | Merge/projection rules. |
| Block no longer required | End reason, authority and effective end. | Explicit release; history remains. | Release timing/notifications. |
| Maintenance completed | Evidence/assessment and Block basis. | Authorized release/correction; no automatic Bookability. | Verification of readiness. |
| Owner changes request | Original Owner intent and new authority/time. | Supersede/amend if authorized. | Existing commitments/conflict. |
| Late Block entry | Event/source time versus entry time. | Record late fact and evaluate effective range. | Retroactive effect and affected Stays. |
| Late correction | Original and corrected truth. | Amendment/supersession/replacement. | Recalculation and visibility. |
| Temporary Commitment expires | Authoritative expiry/effective end. | Derived truth recomputes; no destructive delete. | Expiry/reconciliation policy. |

Correction/release never means “rewrite Availability history”. A later projection may differ because effective truth changed, while historical provenance remains.
