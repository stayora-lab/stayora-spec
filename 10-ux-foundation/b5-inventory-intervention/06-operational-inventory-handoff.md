# B5 — Operational → Inventory Handoff

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Step | Reporter / context | Evidence | Decision owner / authority | Inventory result | Existing truth | Audit / TBD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operational fact observed | Guest, Butler, BQL, Host or authorized Staff | Observation, Unit, range, time, source, issue detail | Reporting capability only; no automatic Inventory Authority | No Inventory mutation by report alone | Stay/Booking/External fact remains unchanged | Preserve reporter/capacity; exact evidence TBD |
| Evidence assessed | Host/Inventory authority or authorized Staff context | Evidence quality, scope, operational impact | Explicit Inventory decision authority | Accept, reject, defer or escalate intervention | Existing commitments/blocks are evaluated | Assessment/reason retained; threshold TBD |
| Maintenance/other Block established | Authorized actor | Decision basis, Unit × Time, reason, effective lifetime | Valid Inventory Authority | Canonical Block contributes to derived Availability | Overlaps preserved; conflict surfaced | Actor/authority/reason/time; release TBD |
| Conflict/exception | Inventory/exception context | Both truths, sources, commitments, evidence | Policy-defined resolver where available | No automatic winner; contextual conflict projection | Booking/External/Stay not silently mutated | Conflict record/resolver TBD |
| Block released/corrected | Authorized actor | Condition ended/correction basis | Authority to end/change original basis | Derived Availability recomputes from effective truth | History remains; no universal Bookability | Release/amendment audit; timing TBD |

The handoff is operational signal → evidence → authorized Inventory decision → canonical basis → derived Availability. Butler/BQL discovery of a problem never substitutes for the decision step.
