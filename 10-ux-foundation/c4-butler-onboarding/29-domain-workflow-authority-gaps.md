# Domain, workflow and authority gaps

| Class | Observed need | Existing concept | Why insufficient / escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| POLICY GAP | Butler eligibility and approval | Application/Eligibility | Criteria and manual operating procedure are not canonical |
| AUTHORITY GAP | Who may assign/reassign and which actions follow | Assignment + Authority Grant | Grantor, scope and non-delegable actions require decision |
| WORKFLOW GAP | Active-Stay replacement and handoff | Assignment validity/history | Propagation, notice and responsibility policy open |
| STATE-MACHINE GAP | Operational milestones versus Stay lifecycle | B4 projections + CP4 Stay | Keep milestones out of Stay states; define policy if needed |
| PRIVACY/PRESENTATION NEED | Need-to-know Guest/Stay projection | CP6 context and privacy principles | Exact fields/consent/retention unresolved |
| DOMAIN GAP | Incident/evidence to Inventory evaluator | B4 Incident + B5 Inventory | Handoff contract/evidence threshold not fixed |
| V0-SCOPE GAP | Manual Butler assignment/coordination | CP5 manual assistance | Confirm pilot procedure without workforce suite |

C4 adds no entity, aggregate, state, permission or HR/workforce concept to close these gaps.
