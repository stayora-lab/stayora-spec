# Need-to-know information boundary

Butler may need assigned Property/Unit, arrival/departure timing, permitted Guest operational contact, Stay status, access/readiness information, assigned responsibilities and Incident facts. Exact fields and privacy implementation remain TBD.

Butler does not automatically see Owner economics, Sale economics, full Payment ledger, Settlement, Payout, unrelated Guest data, identity documents, unrelated Stays/Properties, private authority structure or commercial disputes. Operational visibility is scoped by Assignment, function, Stay lifecycle, Destination policy and privacy policy; visibility never proves authority.
