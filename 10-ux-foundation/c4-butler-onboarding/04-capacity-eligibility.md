# Butler capacity and eligibility

`Butler capacity ≠ Butler eligibility ≠ Butler Assignment`.

“I work as Butler” is a claim or intent. Recognized capacity is an Identity/Party relationship. Operational eligibility is a platform status established through the existing application/eligibility architecture; CP3 records Butler approval/eligibility as distinct from Assignment. Assignment is the scoped operational relationship established by a valid assigner.

C4 does not invent training certification, performance score, staff rank, HR status, employment contract, KYC or workforce lifecycle. Manual-assisted V0 evaluation is acceptable when actor, basis, decision, scope, validity and audit history remain clear. Pending, rejected, suspended or revoked eligibility must not silently create an active Assignment.
