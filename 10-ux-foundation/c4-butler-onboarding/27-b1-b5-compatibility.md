# B1–B5 compatibility

| Baseline | C4 compatibility result |
|---|---|
| B1 Sale-assisted Booking | Butler onboarding does not change Sale commerce or Booking Authority; operational handoff remains downstream of confirmed accommodation. |
| B2 External Booking → Stay | External Accommodation may produce Stay Operations without fabricated Booking; Butler works from Stay/operational truth. |
| B3 Direct Guest Booking | Direct Guest commerce remains independent; Butler context begins only when an operational Stay basis and Assignment exist. |
| B4 Stay Operations | C4 supplies a legitimate Butler actor/Assignment context without changing Stay lifecycle or readiness semantics. |
| B5 Inventory Intervention | Butler evidence may trigger authorized Inventory evaluation but never direct Inventory mutation from Assignment/report alone. |

No accepted journey is redesigned or silently changed.
