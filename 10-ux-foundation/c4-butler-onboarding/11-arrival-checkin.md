# Arrival and Check-in boundary

`Expected Arrival ≠ Physical Arrival ≠ Access ≠ formal Check-in`. Butler may coordinate arrival and record observations within Assignment scope. Check-in is a canonical Stay action and requires explicit operational Check-in authority/evidence where policy requires it; Butler Assignment alone is not enough to infer that authority.

Butler cannot override payment, compliance, access or safety conditions, and C4 does not invent those policies. Arrival observation does not change Stay state automatically, cancel Booking, release Inventory or create a payment result.
