# Incident and evidence boundary

The safe chain is:

```text
Butler observation
  → report / Incident / evidence
  → scoped operational escalation
  → authorized decision by the correct domain authority
  → possible downstream action
```

`Incident ≠ Maintenance Block`; `Evidence ≠ Inventory Authority`; `Report ≠ Finding`; `Finding ≠ Responsibility`; `Responsibility ≠ Consequence`. Butler does not automatically block a Unit, cancel a Booking, refund a Guest, assign blame, deduct an Owner, penalize Sale, or change Verification/Reputation.

Conflicting or incomplete reports preserve all provenance and remain subject to Incident/Quality/Money/Inventory policy owned by the relevant domain.
