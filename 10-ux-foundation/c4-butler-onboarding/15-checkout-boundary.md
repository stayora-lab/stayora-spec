# Checkout boundary

`Physical Departure ≠ Checkout ≠ Stay Completion ≠ Inventory release`. Butler may coordinate departure and record Checkout evidence when explicitly authorized. Checkout alone does not release Inventory, make a Unit Available, complete the Stay, settle money, resolve damage or trigger Reputation/Verification consequences.

Late/early departure, missing access evidence or an unresolved Incident remains an operational exception/policy path. C4 does not create a Checkout override or completion rule.
