# Assignment authority

An assignment requires a legitimate assigning actor/function and matching resource/function scope. CP3/C1 support Host/Primary Host or delegated operational authority, Stayora Admin function and Destination/BQL function only where their authority is explicit. Owner may not assign merely because of ownership; BQL may not assign merely because of destination visibility; no actor may delegate a capability they do not hold.

Assignment establishes the operational relationship. A separate action authority may be needed for Check-in, Checkout, access, evidence handling or other privileged action. The assigner, capacity, authority source, resource, function, validity, reason and time remain auditable. Where the canon is silent about grantor, acceptance or propagation, record `AUTHORITY GAP / TBD`.
