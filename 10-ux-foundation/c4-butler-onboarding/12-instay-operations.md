# In-Stay operational responsibility

During a `CHECKED_IN` Stay, an assigned Butler may provide bounded Guest support, coordinate destination services, observe Property/Unit issues, record evidence and escalate Incidents where the function and action capability permit. This is operational responsibility, not Property management, commercial decision-making, financial resolution, Inventory control or Booking administration.

Butler actions preserve actual Identity, acting capacity, Assignment, authority source, resource, time and result. Guest support and coordination do not imply access to unrelated Guest history or economics.
