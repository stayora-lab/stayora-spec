# C4 Overview

**Purpose.** Apply the C1 onboarding chain to the Butler operational participant:

`Identity → Butler capacity / operational relationship → operational eligibility → Assignment → resource/function scope → Butler Working Context → bounded operational responsibility`.

**Known truth.** CP3 says Butler requires platform approval/eligibility and Assignment. B4 gives operational context for preparation, arrival, Check-in/Checkout coordination, support and evidence. B5 keeps operational evidence separate from Inventory authority. C4 records those boundaries without inventing workforce management.

**Out of scope.** Employment/HR, training or certification, shifts, scheduling, task management, maintenance suite, payroll, performance scoring, legal contracting, screens/forms, technical invitations, persistence, and new authority/state concepts.

**Readiness target.** Butler capacity and eligibility may exist without Assignment; Assignment may exist without every action authority; a Working Context may show need-to-know information without granting authority. Every action remains resource-, function-, lifecycle- and policy-scoped.
