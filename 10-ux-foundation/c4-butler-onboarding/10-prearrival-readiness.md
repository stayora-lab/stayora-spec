# Pre-arrival and readiness boundary

Butler may prepare an assigned Property/Stay, coordinate access and record observations/readiness evidence. `Preparation / READY` is an operational milestone/projection, not a Stay lifecycle state. Readiness does not guarantee Check-in, payment, access or Availability.

Butler may observe and report known conditions; a Host or other authority may be required to confirm a policy milestone or resolve a blocker. C4 does not create a `READY` state, completion blocker or maintenance workflow.
