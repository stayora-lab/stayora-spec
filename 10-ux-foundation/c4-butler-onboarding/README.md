## Protected distinctions

`Identity ≠ Party ≠ Butler capacity ≠ Butler eligibility ≠ Assignment ≠ Working Context ≠ operational responsibility ≠ action authority`. Assignment does not grant Booking, Inventory, commercial, financial, Reputation or Verification consequence authority. Operational evidence is not an Inventory decision; Incident, Finding, Responsibility and Consequence remain separate. Arrival, Check-in, Checkout, Completion and Availability remain distinct.
