# Butler onboarding journey

| Step | Canonical effect | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| BTL1 Resolve Identity and Butler-intending capacity | Identify the actor and Party/capacity | Self-description is not recognized Butler capacity |
| BTL2 Establish operational eligibility where required | Record approval/eligibility outcome | Eligibility is not Assignment or authority |
| BTL3 Propose/establish Assignment | Link the Butler to a resource/function scope with validity and provenance | Assignment is not unrestricted authority |
| BTL4 Activate Butler Working Context | Project assigned operational information | Visibility is not permission |
| BTL5 Perform bounded responsibility | Prepare, coordinate, observe or record evidence where scope allows | Butler cannot control Booking, Inventory, Money or consequences by default |
| BTL6 Handoff operational truth | Route readiness, Incident or completion evidence to the responsible authority | Report does not itself create a Block, Finding or Completion |

Each outcome is independent and can be pending, narrowed, revoked or replaced. There is no universal `BUTLER_ACTIVE` or `BUTLER_ONBOARDED` state.
