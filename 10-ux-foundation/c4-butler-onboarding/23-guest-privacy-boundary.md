# Guest and privacy boundary

Butler may receive the minimum Guest contact and operational information needed for an assigned Stay: arrival/access coordination, support, readiness, Incident and departure context where policy allows. Assignment does not grant Guest history, payment information, identity documents, unrelated Stays, private notes or commercial disputes.

Exact field-level visibility, consent, retention, QR/access linkage and escalation privacy remain policy/UX TBD. Need-to-know is a boundary, not an implementation model.
