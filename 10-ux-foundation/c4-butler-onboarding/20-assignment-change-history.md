# Assignment change, revocation and history

Assignment creation, acceptance where applicable, replacement, temporary substitution, scope correction, expiry, revocation and wrong-resource correction affect future context/actions according to policy. Eligibility can change independently; one capacity can end while other capacities remain.

Historical truth preserves Identity, Butler capacity, Assignment, authority basis, resource/function scope, effective time, action and result. A later revocation or replacement does not rewrite legitimate prior Incident, Check-in, Checkout, evidence or operational actions. Exact propagation during an active Stay, notice, appeal and handoff policy remain TBD.
