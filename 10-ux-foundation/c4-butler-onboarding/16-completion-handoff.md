# Completion handoff

Butler may contribute checkout evidence, observed Property condition, unresolved Incident facts and Guest departure observations to the Stay/Operations context. The canonical Stay completion authority evaluates those facts under CP4/B4 policy. Butler cannot unilaterally complete Stay, settle money, assign damage liability, refund Guest, deduct Owner, change Reputation or change Verification.

Completion readiness is a projection and handoff, not a Butler-owned lifecycle. A post-stay case preserves historical Stay and operational truth rather than rewriting it.
