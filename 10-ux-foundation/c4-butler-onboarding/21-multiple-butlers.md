# Multiple Butler boundary

One Property may have multiple Butlers and a Stay may have multiple operational participants where CP3/B4 permits. Each Assignment has its own scope, validity, responsibility and audit basis. Handoffs and conflicting reports preserve provenance and require operational policy to resolve responsibility.

C4 does not invent a staff hierarchy, primary Butler lifecycle, shift system, work allocation engine or scheduling domain. “Primary” or assisting responsibility is a policy question when the canon is silent.
