# BQL and Destination boundary

Destination/BQL context may consume operational information within legitimate Destination/function scope. Destination visibility does not imply Butler Assignment authority, Inventory Authority, Booking Authority or commercial authority.

If a BQL/Destination function legitimately establishes or changes an Assignment, the grantor, function, resource scope, validity and audit basis must be explicit. Where CP3/C1 do not establish that basis, record `AUTHORITY GAP / TBD` rather than inferring it from visibility.
