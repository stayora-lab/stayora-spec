# Operational to Inventory handoff

The B5-compatible chain is:

`Butler observation → evidence/Incident → authorized Inventory evaluation → Maintenance/Unusable Block or another canonical basis where valid → derived Availability`.

Butler hands off the observed condition, resource/time context, evidence, reporter and operational urgency to the authorized evaluator. Butler may see the downstream status only within need-to-know scope. Butler does not mark unavailable, create/release a Block, override a conflict or mutate Availability unless a separate explicit Inventory Authority exists independent of Butler Assignment.
