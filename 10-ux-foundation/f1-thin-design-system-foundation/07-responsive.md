# F1. Responsive foundation

V0 remains web-based. Responsive behavior preserves task priority and semantic safety; it does not force every surface into one desktop-to-mobile recipe.

| Surface | Responsive emphasis |
|---|---|
| Public Marketplace | Readable discovery, dates, property/unit information and Request intent; progressive disclosure for secondary detail. |
| Guest Stay Access | Mobile-first access to current Stay, arrival/departure and scoped operational information; action labels remain explicit. |
| Host Workspace | Preserve decision context, Unit × Time scope and revalidation signals; dense calendar/list views may adapt by prioritizing rows, ranges or details. |
| Sale Workspace | Preserve supply, relationship, option and Request context; move secondary commercial/provenance detail behind safe disclosure. |
| Operations — Butler | Field-ready readability, connectivity-tolerant presentation and touch-safe actions; do not hide incident/evidence context. |
| Operations — Destination/BQL | Destination-scoped occupancy, access, service and issue information can use dense views; critical attention remains legible on smaller screens. |
| Stayora Admin | Desktop-first density may be appropriate for exception/evidence work, with responsive access to the responsible case rather than an invented mobile admin model. |

Common rules:

- Preserve priority, scope, actor/context and outcome when columns collapse.
- Prefer progressive disclosure, scrolling or detail views over unreadable compressed tables.
- Keep touch targets and focus order safe for consequential actions.
- A responsive projection may rearrange information but must not change authority or canonical truth.
- Do not infer a native-app architecture from field use; that remains outside F1.
