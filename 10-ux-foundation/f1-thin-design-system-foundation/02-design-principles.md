# F1. Design principles

These principles translate CP8-A–E into visual and interaction constraints. They do not add product decisions.

1. **Truth before decoration.** Canonical object truth, provenance and material uncertainty remain understandable at the point of decision. A polished surface cannot hide a stale, partial or external basis.
2. **Authority is visible at the action boundary.** Show whether a person can see, initiate, act, act if authorized, cannot act or has authority that is unknown. An available-looking control is never proof of permission.
3. **Consequence deserves proportionate friction.** High-consequence mutations expose current truth, acting context, scope, authority dependency, preconditions, uncertainty and known outcome. Low-risk navigation stays light.
4. **Context is explicit.** Working Context changes perspective and resource scope; it is not authority. Context switching must not create a second business truth.
5. **Exceptions are first-class.** Conflict, UNKNOWN, manual verification, partial information and reconciliation are represented as meaningful conditions, not generic broken screens.
6. **Shared truth, contextual projection.** A surface may emphasize different facts for its responsibility while preserving object identity, provenance and lifecycle meaning.
7. **Operational clarity.** Operational views prioritize what is happening, where, when, who is responsible, what needs attention and what action is legitimate. This does not create a generic Task domain.
8. **Progressive complexity without hidden risk.** Detail may be progressively disclosed only when omitted detail cannot change the decision. Material authority, uncertainty and consequence remain reachable.
9. **Composition before specialization.** Reusable primitives carry visual behavior; domain patterns compose them only where a repeated responsibility requires a stable presentation.
10. **Accessible by default.** Meaning is available through text, structure, keyboard and assistive technology, not color, hover, motion or density alone.
