# Sale Workspace responsibility

**Users/context:** eligible Sale with active Distribution Relationship and scope.  
**Responsibility:** discover eligible supply; inspect Public Price, real Availability and trust signals; prepare/share options; capture Guest intent; create/monitor Booking Request; monitor attributable Booking/Stay and own economics where supported.

**Actions:** search, compare, quote/share and create Request. Sale does not accept Request, mutate Inventory, see Owner payout/tax/full ledger or become Stay operations. Whitelist is not an unrestricted permission bundle.

**Handoffs:** Lead/intent → Request → Host/Booking Authority; confirmed commerce → Guest Stay Access/Operations; attribution remains separate from commission/Settlement/Payout.

**Partial:** eligible with no Distribution Relationship, relationship with limited scope, or supply visible but not Bookable. No CRM, ranking, advanced dispatch or Affiliate Network.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for Network Adoption and Commerce Validation; full CRM deferred. Source: [CP6 Sale Workspace](../../07-information-architecture/05-sale-workspace.md), C3.
