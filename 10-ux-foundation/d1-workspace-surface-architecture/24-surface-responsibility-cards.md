# Surface responsibility cards

The following cards are the D1 architecture summary. They precede screen design.

| Surface | Primary users/context | Resource scope | Primary tasks | Entry | Excluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public Marketplace | Guest/prospective Guest/Sale | Destination, Property, Unit, public Availability/Price | Discover, compare, Request/consult | Public | Private/economic/operational authority |
| Guest Stay Access | Guest/Staying Party | own Booking/External Stay, Stay, Access | Arrival, access, help, issue, review | Scoped Stay/access basis | Host/Sale/Admin authority |
| Host Workspace | Owner/Host/Co-host | Property, Unit, Inventory, Request, Booking, Stay, cases | Operate supply, decide Request, manage authorized Inventory | Relationship + scope | Unrelated data/automatic authority |
| Sale Workspace | Eligible Sale | scoped supply, Lead, Request, Booking/Stay attribution | Discover, option, Request, monitor | Eligibility + relationship + scope | Booking/Inventory/Stay authority |
| Butler Context | Eligible assigned Butler | assigned Property/Stay/function | Prepare, support, evidence, handoff | Eligibility + Assignment | Commercial/Inventory/financial authority |
| Destination/BQL | Destination Staff/BQL | Destination + operational Property/Stay | Arrivals, access, service, issues | Destination/function | Super-admin/commercial authority |
| Stayora Admin | Staff function | assigned cases/resources | Review, assist, reconcile, audit | Admin function/scope | Universal impersonation/policy shortcut |

Attention inputs, handoffs, policy dependencies and source traces are detailed in [03–23](README.md).
