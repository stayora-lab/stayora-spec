# Navigation responsibility groups

D1 does not finalize menus, labels or routes. It identifies responsibility groups that may later organize navigation:

- Public discovery and destination information;
- Guest Stay, Arrival/Access, Help and Review;
- Host attention, Requests, Bookings/Stays, Properties, Inventory, Issues, People/Access and scoped Money;
- Sale Find, Leads, Requests, Bookings, Earnings and Network;
- Butler Today/Upcoming, Stays and Issues;
- Destination Today/Arrivals/In House/Departures/Access/Services/Issues;
- Admin Attention, Applications/Eligibility, Verification, Incidents, Inventory Conflicts, Money exceptions, Leads, Destinations and Audit.

Groups follow responsibility and handoffs, not database tables. Any additional top-level workspace is an IA/UX gap until canonically justified.
