# Host Workspace responsibility

**Users/context:** Legal Owner, Primary Host, Co-host or other Identity with valid scoped Property/Booking/Inventory/Operations authority. Owner and Host perspectives can overlap but do not collapse.

**Responsibility:** operate Property/Listing; review Requests; make authorized Booking decisions; view/manage Inventory truth; record External Accommodation/Owner Blocks/Maintenance Blocks when separately authorized; coordinate Stays, People/Access, quality and scoped Money.

**Shown:** Property, Unit, Listing, Inventory Commitments/Blocks, derived Availability, Requests, Bookings, Stays, Butler Assignment, Incidents, Verification and authorized financial projections. **Excluded:** unrelated resources, unrestricted Guest data and Sale economics not needed for the task.

**Actions:** edit/publish, accept/reject Request, establish Inventory truth, assign/delegate, coordinate operations and handle exceptions only when effective authority exists. Calendar is a projection of Inventory, not a second calculator.

**Entry/partial:** valid relationship/scope may produce a partial context: Owner without Host authority, Host without publication eligibility, or Property with unresolved relationships. No role shortcut.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for supply, Inventory, Requests, Bookings/Stays, external registration, conflict surfacing and integrity. Source: [CP6 Host Workspace](../../07-information-architecture/04-host-workspace.md), C2.
