# Public Marketplace responsibility

**Users/context:** prospective Guests, Guests and Sale contexts.  
**Responsibility:** discover Destination, Property and Bookable Unit supply; inspect public trust signals, Public Price and derived Availability; enter Direct Guest Request or consultation/Sale path.

**Shown:** public listing/Property, Destination, Unit representation, Public Price, derived Availability, trust/Verified signals and Request entry. **Excluded:** Owner economics, private authority, Inventory conflicts, internal notes, private Guest data and non-public Sale terms.

**Actions:** discover, search, compare, inspect and initiate Request/consultation. Request hands to Host/authorized Booking Authority. Public Marketplace does not accept Requests, mutate Inventory, assign Butler or decide Verification.

**Entry/partial:** public access does not require authenticated relationship; unpublished or incomplete supply may remain private. `Searchable ≠ Available ≠ Bookable`. Non-Verified supply can remain visible when otherwise eligible.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for Commerce Validation and Inventory Trust. Source: [CP6 Public Marketplace](../../07-information-architecture/03-public-and-guest-ia.md), [CP5 journeys](../../06-v0-scope/03-critical-journeys.md).
