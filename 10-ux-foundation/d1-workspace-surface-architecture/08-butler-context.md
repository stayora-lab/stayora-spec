# Butler Context responsibility

**Users/context:** eligible Butler with valid Assignment and Property/Stay/function scope.  
**Responsibility:** view assigned upcoming/current Stays; prepare arrival; report readiness; coordinate arrival/Check-in/Checkout where explicitly authorized; support Guest; record Incident/evidence; hand off operational issues.

**Shown:** assigned Property/Unit, Stay, arrival/departure, access, readiness, permitted Guest operational data and Incident information. **Excluded:** Public Price, payment, Owner/Sale economics, Settlement/Payout, unrelated Stays/Properties and private authority.

Butler evidence is not an Inventory decision. Assignment is not Booking, Inventory, commercial or financial authority. No task/workforce/maintenance suite is created.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for Stay Operations and Destination Stay Coverage. Source: [CP6 Operations](../../07-information-architecture/06-operations-workspaces.md), C4.
