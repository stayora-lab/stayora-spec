# Guest Stay Access responsibility

**Users/context:** Guest or Staying Party member with a valid scoped Booking/External Accommodation → Stay relationship or access basis.  
**Responsibility:** provide relevant confirmation, Stay, arrival, access, Property/Unit, Butler, Destination, help and eligible review information.

**Actions:** view own Stay/access, provide required operational information, use scoped link/QR where canonical, report issue and review when eligible. Guest account is not mandatory where CP5/CP6 permit valid Stay access.

**Shown:** own Booking or external basis where relevant, Stay, arrival/access, operational contact, destination information and issue help. **Excluded:** unrelated Guests/Stays, Owner/Sale economics, internal ledger and private evidence.

**Entry/partial:** valid scoped access relationship; pre-arrival may show preparation/access projection, but access is not Authority. External origin remains visible only where operationally relevant. QR/privacy lifecycle remains TBD.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for Destination Stay Coverage and Commerce Validation. Source: [CP6 Public/Guest IA](../../07-information-architecture/03-public-and-guest-ia.md), [B4](../b4-stay-operations/README.md).
