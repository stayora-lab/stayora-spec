# Empty and partial contexts

Legitimate contexts may have no actionable resource. A Sale may be eligible but lack a Distribution Relationship; Butler may be eligible but unassigned; an Owner may have a Property relationship but no Hosting Authority; a Property may be represented but unpublished; a Host may have a scope with no current Requests/Stays.

The surface should communicate the known relationship, missing outcome and next responsibility without fake data or automatic permissions. D1 does not define copy, CTA design, application forms or onboarding policy.
