# Status presentation principle

Surfaces present canonical lifecycle/status facts with contextual explanation; they do not invent UI statuses or convert milestones into states.

Examples: Request `PENDING/ACCEPTED/REJECTED`; Booking `CONFIRMED`; Payment `UNKNOWN` remains unresolved; Stay `SCHEDULED/CHECKED_IN/CHECKED_OUT/COMPLETED`; Availability is derived; Bookability is contextual; Preparation/Ready is a milestone; onboarding completion is per outcome. Publication, Verification, Managed and external origin remain separate facts.
