# Working Context switching

One Identity may hold Owner/Host, Sale, Butler, Guest and other legitimate capacities. A context is available when its relationship/eligibility/assignment and scope produce a usable projection. Switching context changes perspective, responsibility, visible resource scope and acting-capacity label; it does not grant authority or merge contexts.

Every action re-evaluates Identity, acting capacity, authority basis, resource, lifecycle, policy and need-to-know. A person who is both Sale and Co-host creates a Request in Sale context and accepts only in the separate Co-host/Booking Authority context. A Butler + Host records operational evidence and Host action under separate bases.

No visual switcher, context state or authentication mechanism is designed here.
