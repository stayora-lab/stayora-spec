# Attention and exception model

Attention is a projection of canonical truth, not a generic Task domain or priority algorithm. Examples include pending Request, Inventory conflict, Payment Attempt `UNKNOWN`, Incident, external accommodation conflict, onboarding relationship pending and Stay operational exception.

The owning domain remains responsible: Booking for Request, Inventory for conflict/commitment, Money for unknown payment, Incident/Quality for cases, Identity/Authority for onboarding and Stay/Operations for operational exceptions. Surfaces may summarize, route or acknowledge attention; they may not invent status or resolve policy through presentation.
