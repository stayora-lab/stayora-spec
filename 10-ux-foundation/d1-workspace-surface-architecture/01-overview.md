# D1 Overview

CP6 Information Architecture is the upstream baseline. D1 refines its seven required V0 surfaces without replacing it or adding top-level workspaces:

1. Public Marketplace;
2. Guest Stay Access;
3. Host Workspace;
4. Sale Workspace;
5. Operations — Butler Context;
6. Operations — Destination/BQL Context;
7. Stayora Admin.

Each surface is a responsibility boundary over shared truth. Context availability, visibility and actionability are independent outcomes evaluated against Identity, capacity/relationship, resource scope, lifecycle, policy, need-to-know and CP3 authority. D1 identifies gaps instead of resolving upstream TBDs.
