# Destination / BQL Context responsibility

**Users/context:** Destination Staff/BQL with Destination/function scope.  
**Responsibility:** coordinate destination arrivals, departures, in-house operational occupancy, access, services, Guest/vehicle information where required and operational issues.

**Shown:** Destination, participating Property/Stay operational projections, Arrival/Departure, Access, service and Incident information required by function. **Excluded:** Owner payout/tax, Sale commission, full commercial ledger and unrelated destination/property data.

Destination visibility does not grant Host, Booking, Inventory, Butler Assignment or commercial authority. Any legitimate assignment/escalation requires explicit function and grantor. Oceanami-specific capabilities remain Destination configuration.

**V0:** MUST BUILD for Destination Stay Coverage and Network Adoption. Source: [CP6 Operations](../../07-information-architecture/06-operations-workspaces.md), C4.
