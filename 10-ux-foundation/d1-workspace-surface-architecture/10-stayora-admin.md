# Stayora Admin responsibility

**Users/context:** Stayora staff with explicit domain/function/resource authority.  
**Responsibility:** assist onboarding/eligibility, Verification cases, Incidents, Inventory conflicts, Money/Settlement exceptions, Leads, Destination configuration and audit.

**Actions:** assess, assign, record evidence, apply explicit policy action, reconcile, correct through new fact/adjustment and audit. Admin does not impersonate Owner/Host/Sale/Guest, choose unresolved winners or become universal business authority.

**Shown:** scoped case evidence, source, status/outcome, reason, authority basis and audit history. **Excluded:** unrelated private data and unrestricted ledgers. Manual assistance remains auditable and domain-owned.

**V0:** MANUAL-ASSISTED where CP5 allows; no super-admin shortcut or generic case/task platform. Source: [CP6 Admin](../../07-information-architecture/07-admin-workspace.md).
