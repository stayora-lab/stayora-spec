# C1 — Shared Onboarding Reasoning Model

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

Every onboarding proposal should answer these questions without collapsing concepts:

| Question | Architecture answer |
|---|---|
| Who is this? | Resolve or establish an Identity; do not duplicate an existing person for each role. |
| What Party/capacity are they acting as? | Represent Individual/Organization Party and capacity/acting capacity where applicable. |
| What relationship is being established? | Identify the relationship to a Party, Property, Unit, Destination, Stay or distribution context. |
| To which resource? | Name explicit resource scope: Destination, Property, Bookable Unit, Booking, Stay or relevant network scope. |
| Who may establish/confirm it? | Use valid grantor, Admin function or self-entry basis; invitation/claim alone is insufficient. |
| What evidence/basis is required? | Preserve source, evidence and policy status; do not invent thresholds. |
| What eligibility applies? | Separate platform eligibility from relationship and resource authority. |
| What context becomes available? | Project the relevant Working Context; visibility does not grant all actions. |
| What authority follows? | Only explicit capability/grant/assignment in scope. |
| What authority does not follow? | Record non-inherited Owner, Host, Booking, Inventory, Money, operations and consequence boundaries. |
| What remains pending? | Keep unresolved evidence, approval, assignment, publication or verification as separate open outcomes. |
| What can the subject do next? | Describe product outcome at a conceptual level; do not design a screen flow. |

## No universal status

Identity established, relationship established, resource associated, assignment active, authority granted, context usable, Property represented, publication-eligible and operationally ready are independent outcomes. C1 creates no universal onboarding state machine.
