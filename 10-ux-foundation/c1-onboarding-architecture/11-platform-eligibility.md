# C1 — Platform Eligibility Mapping

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Concept | Meaning | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Known Identity | Stayora can identify/represent an actor. | Eligible, trusted or authorized. |
| Platform Eligibility | Actor/resource may participate in a platform capability under policy. | Commercial relationship or resource authority. |
| Commercial Relationship | Sale/Host/Distribution relationship in a scope. | Platform approval alone or Inventory/Booking authority. |
| Resource Authority | Explicit capability over Property/Unit/Booking/Stay/Inventory. | Ownership, context visibility or assignment alone. |
| Property publication eligibility | Property meets conditions to publish. | Property Verified, Owner authority or Managed. |
| Verification eligibility/status | Property-supply quality program/status. | Identity verification or universal account approval. |
| Operational eligibility | Butler/Destination actor may be assigned/operate under platform/destination policy. | Booking, pricing, Inventory or finance authority. |

Canonical sources support platform eligibility for Sale, Butler and Property/supply contexts. Exact approval, suspension, verification and appeal policies remain open. Verification owns supply-quality programs; Identity & Authority owns identity verification/platform eligibility.
