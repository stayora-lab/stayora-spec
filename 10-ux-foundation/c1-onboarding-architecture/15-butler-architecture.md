# C1 — Butler Architecture

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

## Required conceptual prerequisites for later Butler onboarding

1. Identity and Butler capacity/platform eligibility are represented.
2. A valid assigning actor/relationship can establish a Butler Assignment.
3. Assignment names the Property/Stay/Destination/function scope and validity.
4. The Butler Working Context projects assigned operational information.
5. Operational capability is evaluated separately from assignment/visibility.

## Butler boundary

Assignment may enable preparation, arrival/Check-in/Checkout coordination, Guest support, readiness/evidence and Incident reporting. It does not automatically grant Booking acceptance, pricing, Inventory, payment/refund, Settlement/Payout, Owner, financial consequence or Reputation/Verification consequence authority.

Assignment change/revocation affects future context/actions and preserves prior legitimate history. Exact grants, replacement, propagation, local destination limits and privacy fields remain TBD. C1 does not design workforce management or Butler forms.
