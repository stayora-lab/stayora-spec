# C1 — Onboarding Completion Outcomes

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

There is no universal `ONBOARDED` outcome. Later flows may independently reach:

| Outcome | Meaning | Independent from |
|---|---|---|
| Identity established | Person/account is represented. | Party, eligibility, resource authority. |
| Party represented | Individual/Organization Party relationship exists. | Identity duplicate, Owner/Host authority. |
| Relationship established | Subject/resource or Party relationship is accepted. | Capability/permission to act. |
| Resource associated | Identity/Party is linked to Destination/Property/Unit/Stay scope. | Publication, Verification, Inventory authority. |
| Platform eligible | Actor/resource may participate in a platform capability. | Commercial relationship or resource authority. |
| Assignment active | Butler/Staff/Co-host assignment is valid in scope/time. | Booking, Inventory or finance authority. |
| Authority granted | Explicit capability/grant is effective. | All other capabilities or contexts. |
| Context usable | Working Context can project relevant resources/actions. | Every action permission. |
| Property represented | Property/Units exist in canonical structure. | Published, Verified or Managed. |
| Publication-eligible | Property may publish under policy. | Verified, Owner, Host or Available. |
| Operationally ready | Actor/resource can perform scoped operational work. | Commercial authority or full onboarding completion. |

These are conceptual outcomes, not a new global state machine.
