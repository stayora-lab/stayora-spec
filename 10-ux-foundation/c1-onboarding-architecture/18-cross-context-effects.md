# C1 — Cross-Context Effects

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Milestone | Subject sees | Grantor/assigner sees | Resource/context change | Authority change | Does not change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identity established | Own identity/contact outcome. | Staff may see scoped identity resolution. | Identity can be referenced. | None by itself. | Party, eligibility, resource authority. |
| Owner relationship established | Owner relationship/status where allowed. | Authority/evidence outcome. | Property association may be linked. | Only explicit Owner-related authority. | Host, Booking, Inventory, Payout. |
| Host relationship established | Hosting context/resource scope. | Hosting responsibility/authority basis. | Property/Unit context. | Hosting capabilities per grant. | Legal ownership, every finance capability. |
| Sale relationship established | Sale context and eligible supply. | Commercial relationship/attribution scope. | Distribution context. | Sale capabilities only. | Booking acceptance, Inventory, operations. |
| Butler assignment established | Assigned operational context. | Assignment scope/validity. | Property/Stay/Destination operational projection. | Operational capabilities per grant. | Commercial authority. |
| Property represented | Resource/property context. | Property/resource evidence. | Property/Units/Destination relationship. | None unless separately granted. | Publication, Verified, Managed. |
| Authority granted | Action/resource scope becomes usable. | Grant/evidence/audit basis. | Effective permission context. | Specific capability only. | Other capabilities, unrelated resources. |
| Authority revoked | Restricted future action/context. | Revocation/effective time. | Scope/visibility may reduce. | Future authority removed. | Historical valid actions. |
