# C1 — Sale Architecture

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

## Required conceptual prerequisites for later Sale onboarding

1. Identity/Party and Sale capacity are represented.
2. Platform eligibility/approval is established under Identity & Authority.
3. Relevant Distribution Relationship to a Commercial Authority holder/scope exists where required.
4. Sale Working Context can search eligible supply, inspect Availability/Public Price, prepare an Offer and create/monitor Booking Requests.
5. Attribution/economics are recorded only when genuinely attributable.

## Explicit exclusions

Sale capacity alone does not grant Booking Authority, Host Authority, Inventory Authority, pricing ownership, payment/refund authority, settlement/payout authority or Stay operational authority. Whitelist is not a permission bundle; Affiliate is distinct from Sale. C1 does not design Lead Distribution or Sale application screens.

## Multi-capacity case

If one Identity is both Sale and Co-host, a privileged acceptance or Inventory action is valid only under the explicit Co-host authority context and scope, with acting capacity audited. The Sale context remains a different perspective.
