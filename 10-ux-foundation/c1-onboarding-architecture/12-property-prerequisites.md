# C1 — Property Onboarding Prerequisites

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

C1 defines prerequisites for a later Property flow; it does not design forms or screens.

| Outcome | Conceptual prerequisite | Does not automatically follow |
|---|---|---|
| Property represented | Property identity/existence and authorized representation. | Ownership, Hosting, publication or Verification. |
| Destination membership | Valid relationship to a Destination/context. | BQL/commercial authority or global platform scope. |
| Owner relationship | Party/Identity claim plus evidence/policy. | Primary Host, Inventory, Booking or Payout. |
| Host relationship | Hosting authority/relationship and resource scope. | Legal title or financial beneficiary. |
| Bookable Units represented | Property structure/capacity and Unit identity. | Availability, Commitment, publication or Bookability. |
| Listing/publication eligibility | Property content/compliance and applicable platform policy. | Verified status or Managed. |
| Inventory responsibility | Explicit Inventory capability/authority over Unit × Time. | Owner/Host identity alone or operational observation. |
| Verification program | Supply-quality application/evidence/inspection where applicable. | Identity verification, publication by itself or luxury. |
| Commercial relationship | Distribution/Booking context where legitimate. | Property creation, Owner relationship or Host authority. |

Canonical distinctions remain: `Property exists ≠ published`, `published ≠ Verified`, `Verified ≠ Managed`, and `Owner Listed ≠ Verified ≠ Managed`. Stayora Managed remains outside current core.
