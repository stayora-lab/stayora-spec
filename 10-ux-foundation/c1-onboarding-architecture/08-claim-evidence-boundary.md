# C1 — Claim and Evidence Boundary

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Claim | Required conceptual separation | Safe result |
|---|---|---|
| “I own this Property” | Claim → evidence → ownership relationship → authority evaluation. | Pending/disputed/accepted relationship; Owner does not automatically become Host/Primary Host. |
| “I host this Villa” | Claim → hosting relationship/authority basis → resource scope. | Hosting context only where valid; not legal ownership or finance. |
| “I work as Sale” | Identity/capacity → platform eligibility → Distribution Relationship/attribution. | Sale context only when eligible; no Host/Booking/Inventory authority. |
| “I am assigned Butler” | Claim → assignment source/grantor → active assignment. | Operational context only when valid; no commercial authority. |
| “I represent this organization” | Identity → Party representation → authority basis. | Organization relationship subject to policy; no enterprise organization system is created. |

A claim is not truth, evidence is not necessarily authority, and authority is scoped. C1 does not invent verification workflows, evidence thresholds or legal conclusions. Where sources are silent, classify as policy/authority TBD.
