# C1 — Relationship Architecture

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Relationship | Subject / resource | What it can establish conceptually | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity ↔ Party representation | Identity acting for Individual/Organization Party | Acting capacity/subject representation where valid. | Legal ownership, finance or every Party action. |
| Ownership relationship | Owner Party ↔ Property | Ownership-related claim/relationship subject to evidence/policy. | Primary Host, Booking Authority, Inventory Authority or Payout. |
| Hosting relationship | Host/Primary Host ↔ Property/Unit | Hosting responsibility/authority anchor where valid. | Legal title, Financial Beneficiary or unlimited delegation. |
| Co-host delegation | Co-host Identity ↔ scoped Property/Unit/Booking/Stay | Explicit delegated capabilities in resource/lifecycle scope. | Primary Host powers, unrestricted delegation or finance. |
| Sale Distribution Relationship | Sale ↔ Commercial Authority holder/scope | Discovery/Offer/Request/attribution context under eligibility. | Booking acceptance, Inventory, Host, payment/refund or operations authority. |
| Butler Assignment | Butler Identity ↔ Property/Stay/operational scope | Operational context and scoped Stay information. | Booking, pricing, Inventory, payment, Owner or consequence authority. |
| Destination Staff relationship | Staff Identity ↔ Destination/function | Destination-scoped operational context. | Global BQL or commercial authority. |
| Guest relationship | Guest Identity/Party ↔ Booking/Stay | Scoped participation, access and operational actions. | Account requirement, payment authority or Host authority. |

Relationships are temporal and may be revoked/changed. A relationship can influence effective permission but is not itself necessarily Authority.
