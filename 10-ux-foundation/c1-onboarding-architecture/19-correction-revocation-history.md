# C1 — Correction, Revocation and Historical Truth

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Change | Preserve | Safe effect |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong relationship | Original actor, resource, basis, time and relationship claim. | Correct/supersede future relationship; do not rewrite prior valid actions. |
| Wrong Property/Unit association | Original scope and evidence. | Amend/reassign only under valid authority; preserve affected history. |
| Duplicate Identity linkage | Existing identity/source and attempted duplicate. | Resolve/merge only under explicit policy; do not silently duplicate or erase. |
| Authority granted incorrectly | Grantor, grant, actor, scope, effective time and actions performed. | Revoke future authority; route past actions to policy review if needed. |
| Authority revoked | Revocation basis/effective time. | Restrict future actions; historical valid actions remain valid. |
| Owner/Host changes | Prior and new relationships/authority. | Apply future scope/time rules; no destructive Booking/Stay rewrite. |
| Butler reassignment | Prior/current assignment and effective time. | Change future operational context; preserve prior evidence. |
| Sale relationship ends | Distribution relationship, attribution and effective time. | Stop future attribution/context; preserve completed history/economics under policy. |

Correction, supersession, replacement and explicit revocation preserve provenance. C1 does not design persistence or retroactive consequence rules.
