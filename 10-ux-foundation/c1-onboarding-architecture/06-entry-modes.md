# C1 — Entry Modes

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Entry mode | Initiator | Subject/resource | Relationship proposed | Authority/acceptance | Evidence/result | Failure/TBD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-entry | Person/Party | Identity, capacity or resource claim | Self-declared relationship/context | Self-entry does not prove authority; later evidence/policy applies. | Pending Identity/relationship/eligibility outcome. | Duplicate identity, claim evidence and approval. |
| Invitation | Authorized actor | Intended recipient + resource/context | Proposed relationship/assignment | Grantor may invite; recipient acceptance is not authority by itself. | Acceptance can allow evaluation/relationship establishment. | Wrong recipient, revoked invitation, expiry/mechanism TBD. |
| Authorized assignment | Host/Primary Host/Destination authority | Butler/Co-host/Staff + resource | Assignment/delegation relationship | Grantor needs authority; assigned subject may need eligibility/acceptance. | Active scoped assignment if valid. | Grant/revocation/propagation TBD. |
| Stayora/Admin-assisted | Authorized Staff function | Person/Party/resource | Identity, relationship or resource representation | Staff function/resource authority and audit required. | Manual-assisted outcome; no universal Admin power. | Evidence, privacy, appeal and escalation TBD. |
| Existing relationship discovered/claimed | Existing actor or system source | Existing resource/Party | Claim/reconciliation of relationship | Claim is not truth; authorized review/evidence required. | Preserve existing and claimed truth. | Dispute/duplicate/precedence TBD. |
| Property-first | Authorized resource actor | Property/Unit before actor relationship is complete | Property representation first | Property authority/evidence separate from later Host/Owner onboarding. | Property can remain represented but not published/operable. | Missing Host/Owner/Verification/publication. |
| Actor-first | Identity/Party before resource exists | Capacity/eligibility first | Actor may later associate to resource | Eligibility does not grant resource authority. | Context may exist without resource actions. | Association and scope TBD. |

C1 does not require one universal entry mode or a universal onboarding sequence.
