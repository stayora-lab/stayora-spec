# C1 — Onboarding Architecture Overview

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

## Purpose

Onboarding is the coordinated establishment of identity, Party representation, relationships, resource associations, eligibility, assignments, authority and usable contexts. It is not one global `onboarded` boolean and not a `choose role → get permissions` flow.

## Core question

How does a real person or organization become legitimately able to operate in a Stayora Working Context against the correct resources with the correct authority?

## Protected distinctions

`Identity ≠ Party ≠ Account`; `Role/Capacity ≠ Authority`; `Relationship ≠ Authority`; `Ownership ≠ Hosting Authority`; `Working Context ≠ Permission`; `Assignment ≠ Commercial Authority`; `Invitation ≠ Authority`; `Claim ≠ Evidence ≠ Truth`; `Account creation ≠ onboarding completion`; `Property existence ≠ publication ≠ Verification ≠ Managed`.

## C1 outcome

C1 supplies reusable reasoning for later onboarding journeys. It does not choose authentication, evidence thresholds, approval workflow, forms, screens, technical RBAC, universal KYC, organization management or detailed actor flow.
