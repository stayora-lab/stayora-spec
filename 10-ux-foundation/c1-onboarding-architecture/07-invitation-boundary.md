# C1 — Invitation Boundary

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

```text
Authorized actor
  → invitation/proposal
  → intended recipient
  → identity resolution/establishment
  → acceptance (where required)
  → relationship/assignment establishment
  → authority only where explicitly granted
```

## Non-equivalence

`Invitation ≠ Identity`, `Invitation ≠ Relationship`, `Invitation ≠ Authority`, `Invitation ≠ Assignment completion`.

An authorized grantor may invite a Butler, Co-host, Destination Staff member or other intended participant. Acceptance identifies consent/participation; it does not silently create Booking Authority, Inventory Authority, finance, Owner status or commercial power.

## Open boundaries

Recipient identity resolution, wrong-recipient handling, revocation, expiry, notification, contact mechanism and acceptance evidence are TBD. C1 does not choose email, OTP, magic link, token, password or provider.
