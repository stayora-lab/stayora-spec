# CP8-A — V0 Onboarding Prerequisites

> Status: **ACCEPTED AS CP8-B BASELINE** · **NOT FROZEN**

This is a minimum prerequisite model for V0 participation. It identifies what must be true before a Host/Property/Sale/Butler can take part in a critical journey. It does not design forms, steps, KYC, legal/compliance policy or approval automation.

## Host / Owner prerequisite

Before the Host/Owner participates in a V0 supply or commerce journey:

- a Stayora Identity and the relevant Party/ownership or operating relationship are represented;
- a valid Primary Host or delegated authority basis exists for the Property/Bookable Unit actions the person will perform;
- required platform eligibility and any publish/compliance gate that already exists in canonical policy is satisfied;
- the actor can see and act on the scoped Property, Inventory, Request, Booking or Stay responsibility assigned to them;
- the actor understands that ownership, hosting authority, financial beneficiary and payout authority are separate;
- an external commitment can be recorded only by an actor with explicit Inventory Authority.

**Open.** Authority evidence, transfer precedence, non-delegable capabilities, legal/KYC and exact onboarding policy remain TBD/legal-validation-required where documented.

## Property / Bookable Unit prerequisite

Before a Property participates in discovery, Inventory or a V0 Stay:

- the Property is represented and linked to a Destination where destination membership is required;
- a V0 Bookable Unit exists with a valid scope; for Oceanami, the conceptual simplification is one Villa = one Property plus one Entire-Villa Unit;
- listing/supply information and the One Public Price required for the supported journey are available under valid authority;
- the effective Inventory/Availability source and authorized commitment/block actions are known;
- the Host/Co-host relationship and any required publish/eligibility condition are represented;
- quality/Verification is represented separately from marketplace participation and is not silently treated as identity verification.

**Open.** Detailed listing completeness, Verification standard, destination integrations, nested inventory and legal right-to-operate evidence remain policy/product TBD.

## Sale prerequisite

Before a Sale participates in V0 commerce:

- the Identity has submitted the Sale application and reached the canonical eligible/approved condition required by policy;
- a valid Sale–Host Distribution Relationship and transaction context exist for the supply the Sale may use;
- the Sale can search relevant Availability, Public Price and trust signals within scope;
- the Sale can create/monitor a Booking Request but does not receive Host Booking Authority by role label;
- any attributed economics are scoped to the Sale's own entitlement projection and do not expose Owner ledger by default;
- a manual lead/assignment path can be used where CP5 marks advanced distribution as deferred.

**Open.** Approval detail, Affiliate relationship/economics, lead timing/dispatch, discount limits/funding and exact earnings visibility remain TBD/WORKING MODEL.

## Butler prerequisite

Before a Butler participates in V0 operations:

- the Identity has submitted the Butler application and reached the required eligibility/approval condition;
- a valid assignment connects the Butler to the relevant Property/Stay and operational scope;
- the Butler can access the need-to-know arrival, readiness, access, Staying Party and issue information for assigned work;
- the Butler can record operational evidence/Incident without being granted Booking, Inventory, financial or reputation-consequence authority;
- assignment and access remain distinguishable so a role approval does not imply access to every Stay.

**Open.** Assignment changes, staffing relationships, local access policy, Guest data fields and escalation rules remain TBD.

## Boundary for later onboarding UX

Later work may turn these prerequisites into interactions only after preserving:

- identity, eligibility, relationship, authority and assignment as separate concepts;
- no silent legal/KYC conclusion;
- no new actor or permission bundle;
- auditability of approval, delegation and assignment decisions;
- V0 alignment with the four CP5 validation dimensions.
