# Sale and Stay Operations boundary

Sale commerce involvement does not make Sale an operational actor. After Booking confirmation, operational truth hands off to the Host, Butler assignment, Destination/BQL functions and Guest contexts under B4. Sale may retain limited commercial/relevant visibility where canonical policy allows.

Sale does not receive Check-in, Checkout, Butler responsibility, Incident consequence authority, access-control authority or broad Guest operational data merely from Sale participation. Need-to-know data sharing and any manual assistance remain policy/operations TBD. External Accommodation and Stay contexts remain compatible with B2; Booking is not the only source of Stay truth.
