# Detailed conceptual Sale onboarding step matrix

This is a reasoning matrix, not a form, screen, API or persistence model.

| Step / context | Intent and entry condition | Identity / capacity / eligibility | Relationship / scope / basis | Authority and action | Domain/context effects | Failure / policy/TBD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Identity resolution | Identity enters self, invitation or Admin-assisted path | Identity; Party; Sale-intending capacity | Claim/application basis | Current onboarding function only | Capacity proposal; no Sale authority | Matching and organization policy TBD |
| S2 Application/intent | Actor seeks Sale participation | Capacity proposed; eligibility not yet satisfied | Application provenance | Applicant may submit; reviewer scope TBD | Application outcome only | Approval criteria/evidence TBD |
| S3 Eligibility decision | Application is reviewed | Eligibility pending/ELIGIBLE/REJECTED etc. | Platform status; no Distribution Relationship yet | Authorized eligibility function | Eligibility projection | Manual procedure, suspension/revoke policy |
| S4 Relationship establishment | Eligible Sale seeks supply relationship | Sale capacity + valid eligibility | Sale↔Commercial Authority holder; scope/validity | Distribution Authority/holder policy | Relationship and potential capability basis | Grantor, terms, scope and activation TBD |
| S5 Context activation | Actor needs discover/request capability | Eligibility valid; relationship active | Resource/market scope | Effective Permission evaluation | Sale Working Context projection | Scope/privacy policy |
| S6 Supply discovery | Sale searches relevant supply | Active context | Property/Unit scope | Read/search capability | Public/Sale visibility; Availability consumed | Search/Availability/Bookability boundaries |
| S7 Option and intent | Sale prepares option and captures Guest need | Active context | Request/Lead basis and attribution candidate | Sale may prepare/create Request if allowed | Offer/Request intent; no Booking | Offer ownership/minimum fields/notification TBD |
| S8 Request creation | Sufficient Guest intent and current supply truth | Active eligibility/relationship/scope | Sale attribution candidate | Create Booking Request, not accept | Request PENDING; Host handoff | Request policy and authority revalidation |
| S9 Downstream outcome | Host/Booking Authority decides | Sale context remains observer/attributor | Attribution and relationship history | Only independent Booking Authority accepts | Booking/Stay/Money downstream; no Sale authority | Payment, attribution and operations policies |

For every step, audit should retain actual Identity, acting capacity, basis/source, resource scope, action, time and result as required by CP7; this does not choose a physical history mechanism.
