# Sale entry modes

| Mode | Initiator / subject | Proposed relationship | Eligibility / authority basis | Result and pending boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-entry/application | Identity or Party representative / self | Sale capacity/application | Identity resolution and Sale approval policy | Application pending/approved/rejected; no relationship yet unless established |
| Invitation | Authorized Stayora or commercial context / invited Identity | Sale capacity or relationship proposal | Invitation plus eligibility/acceptance policy | Consent does not alone grant authority |
| Admin-assisted | Stayora staff / Identity and Party | Capacity, eligibility or relationship setup | Staff function, reason and audit | Admin does not impersonate Sale or Commercial Authority holder |
| Existing Identity becomes Sale | Existing Identity / new Sale capacity | Independent Sale capacity | Eligibility review | Other capacities remain unchanged |
| Owner/Host also becomes Sale | Identity already Owner/Host / Sale capacity | Sale relationship separate from supply relationship | Sale eligibility and distribution policy | Ownership/hosting does not imply Sale eligibility |
| Existing Sale relationship | Existing eligible Identity / new resource scope | Additional Distribution Relationship/scope | Commercial Authority holder and policy | Existing relationship does not widen scope automatically |

Technical invitation/authentication, approval criteria, evidence thresholds, contracting and market-wide scope remain TBD where not canonical.
