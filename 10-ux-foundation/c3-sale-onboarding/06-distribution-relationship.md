# Distribution Relationship

The effective conceptual capability is derived from:

`Sale Platform Eligibility + Sale↔Commercial Authority Relationship + Property Distribution Policy + Transaction Context + Lead/Attribution Context`.

The relationship is conceptually Sale ↔ Commercial Authority holder ↔ Authority Scope, not automatically a Property-wide or Stayora-wide partner contract. It has scope, lifecycle, provenance and audit history. CP4 recognizes NORMAL, WHITELIST and BLACKLIST relationship states:

- **NORMAL** may permit discovery/compare/quote/request subject to policy.
- **WHITELIST** may unlock Instant Book only when all other property, platform and transaction conditions pass; it is not unrestricted authority.
- **BLACKLIST** is relationship-scoped, does not globally suspend Sale, and does not erase history.

Who establishes a relationship, exact scope, commercial terms, activation/termination and appeal remain policy/workflow TBD unless a canonical source establishes them. Distribution Relationship never becomes Booking or Inventory Authority.
