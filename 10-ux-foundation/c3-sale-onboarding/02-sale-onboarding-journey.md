# Sale onboarding journey

| Step | Canonical effect | What does not follow |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Resolve Identity and Party/capacity | Identify the actor and Sale-intending capacity | Registration is not commercial authorization |
| S2 Submit Sale intent/application or enter through invitation/Admin assistance | Preserve a proposal and provenance | Self-description is not recognized Sale capacity |
| S3 Establish platform eligibility where required | Record the CP4 eligibility outcome | Eligibility is not a Distribution Relationship or authority |
| S4 Establish Distribution Relationship | Link eligible Sale to a Commercial Authority holder/scope under relationship policy | Relationship is not Booking/Inventory authority |
| S5 Resolve resource/market scope | Project only the supply/market context supported by relationship and policy | All supply is not automatically visible/bookable |
| S6 Activate Sale Working Context | Make bounded discovery, option preparation and Request actions useful | Context does not grant permission |
| S7 Attribute eligible activity where canonical | Preserve attributable Sale handling/acquisition context | Attribution is not commission, settlement or payout |

Each outcome can remain pending or be revoked independently. A Sale relationship may exist before any Request; no Booking, Inventory Commitment or Stay is created by onboarding.
