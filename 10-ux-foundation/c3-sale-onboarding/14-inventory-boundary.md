# Sale and Inventory boundary

Sale may consume derived Availability and Bookability information within its legitimate supply scope. Sale does not gain authority to create Owner Blocks, Maintenance/Unusable Blocks, commitments, release or override conflicts, or mutate Availability merely because it distributes accommodation.

The B5 chain remains `basis/evidence → explicit Inventory Authority → Commitment/Block truth → derived Availability`. Any Sale actor who separately holds an explicit Inventory capability acts under that separate basis and scope; the Sale relationship itself is not enough. C3 does not design calendar controls or conflict policy.
