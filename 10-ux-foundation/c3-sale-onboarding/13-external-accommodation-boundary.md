# Sale and External Accommodation

Under B2, Sale may report or submit an off-platform accommodation fact where the workflow permits. Reporting information is not authoritative External Accommodation truth, and neither is automatically an Inventory Commitment.

Only an actor with explicit Inventory Authority and the relevant resource scope may authoritatively record an External Accommodation or establish the corresponding Inventory Commitment. Sale eligibility, Distribution Relationship, whitelist or Request attribution alone are insufficient. External commerce may establish Inventory truth and/or a Stay without creating a Stayora Booking. C3 does not change B2 or invent evidence/approval criteria for reports.
