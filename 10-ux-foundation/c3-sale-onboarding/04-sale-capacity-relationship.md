# Sale capacity and relationship

Sale capacity is a recognized capacity of an Identity/Party to act in a Sale context. A statement “I am a Sale” is a claim or intent. Platform-recognized capacity requires the existing application/eligibility architecture; C3 does not invent approval criteria.

`Sale capacity ≠ Sale eligibility ≠ Distribution Relationship`. Eligibility is a platform status. Distribution Relationship is a scoped commercial relationship between Sale and a Commercial Authority holder/authority scope. It may be NORMAL, WHITELIST or BLACKLIST under CP4 policy, with temporal validity, provenance and audit history.

The relationship does not create Host, Co-host, Booking, Inventory, payment, settlement, payout or Stay authority. It may influence effective distribution capability together with Property Distribution Policy, Transaction Context and Lead/Attribution Context.
