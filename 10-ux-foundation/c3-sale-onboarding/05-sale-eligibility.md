# Platform eligibility

CP4 defines the conceptual application and eligibility boundaries:

```text
Application: SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED / REJECTED / WITHDRAWN
Eligibility: ELIGIBLE ↔ SUSPENDED → REVOKED
```

`APPROVED` is an application outcome; `ELIGIBLE` is the effective platform status. Sale Eligibility is separate from Sale↔Commercial Authority Relationship and Transaction Eligibility. Reinstatement after revoke requires an explicit auditable process or new application as applicable.

Manual-assisted V0 evaluation is compatible with CP5 when the actor, basis, decision, scope and history remain auditable. C3 does not define scores, rankings, KYC, training, tiers, performance thresholds or universal approval. Pending, denied, suspended or revoked eligibility must not silently activate a Sale Context or preserve future actions beyond policy.
