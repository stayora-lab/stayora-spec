# Sale + Co-host stress test

Identity A may simultaneously have:

- Sale capacity and an active Distribution Relationship;
- a Co-host relationship to Property X;
- explicit delegated Booking Authority for Property X.

As Sale, A may discover supply and create a Request within Sale scope. A may accept that Request only in a separate Co-host Working Context when the delegated Booking Authority, resource scope, lifecycle and policy are valid. The audit records the actual Identity, acting capacity, authority source, resource and time.

Sale creation of the Request does not authorize acceptance. Co-host delegation does not widen Sale scope or create Inventory, finance or Stay operations authority. If the delegation is revoked, Sale capacity and any valid attribution may remain independently active.
