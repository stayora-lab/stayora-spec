# Commercial information boundary

Sale may need public fit, Public Price, real Availability, trust signals and the information required to prepare/share an option or create a Request, as established by CP6/B1. Direct Guest and Sale paths use the same Public Price; a Sale-funded discount does not silently reduce Owner entitlement.

Keep separate:

- Public/Guest Listed Price;
- Sale-facing terms, only when a canonical policy makes them visible;
- final transaction/commercial snapshot;
- Owner economics, tax, profitability and full Owner ledger;
- Sale economic entitlement;
- Settlement and Payout.

C3 does not expose Owner economics merely because someone is Sale, and does not invent a pricing formula, commission percentage, payout rule, CBV calculation or financial visibility grant. Sale base distribution commission is confirmed at 10% of Commissionable Booking Value in CP7; exact CBV and entitlement conditions remain policy-dependent and are not redesigned here.
