# Multiple Sale and attribution conflict

Potential conflicts include Sale A creating the initial Request, Sale B later assisting, a Guest changing Sale contact, duplicate attribution, an expired relationship during the journey, or a Host receiving more than one Sale path.

C3 preserves each claim, relationship state, timestamp, source, communication/evidence basis and downstream object. It does not select an attribution winner, invent a commission split, or rewrite valid history. Request/Booking truth and authority remain independent of the attribution dispute. The policy owner must define qualification, replacement, conflict, fraud and appeal behavior before detailed interaction or money automation.
