# Revocation, change and historical truth

Eligibility suspension/revocation, Distribution Relationship termination, scope changes, Sale departure/rejoin, incorrect linkage, Sale-to-Host changes and Co-host revocation affect future context and actions according to policy. They do not destructively rewrite valid past Requests, Bookings, attribution, commitments or completed Stays.

The conceptual history preserves Identity, acting capacity, relationship/grant source, resource scope, effective validity, action, reason/basis, time and result. Rejoining requires the applicable eligibility/relationship process; it does not resurrect all former scope automatically. Exact notice, appeal, effective-time and attribution correction policies remain TBD.
