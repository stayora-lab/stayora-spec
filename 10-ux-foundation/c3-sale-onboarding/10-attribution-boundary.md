# Attribution boundary

Attribution records which Sale context handled or sourced an activity when the canonical journey and policy support that fact. It is separate from authority and money:

`Sale attribution ≠ Booking Authority ≠ commission entitlement ≠ Settlement ≠ Payout`.

Attribution may attach to a Lead, Request, Booking or qualified commercial outcome where CP4/CP7 policy allows. Acquisition Attribution (for example Affiliate) and Sales Handling Attribution may coexist; Sale assistance, replacement or remediation does not silently erase valid prior attribution. If multiple Sales claim an activity, preserve each claim, basis, timestamp and relationship state; C3 does not choose a winner or invent a commission rule. Ended relationships do not rewrite legitimate historical attribution.
