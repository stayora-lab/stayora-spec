# Sale onboarding completion outcomes

C3 does not create `SALE_ONBOARDED = true`. Independent outcomes are:

1. Identity established;
2. Sale capacity recognized;
3. eligibility satisfied or pending/revoked;
4. Distribution Relationship established and active;
5. resource/market scope resolved;
6. Sale Working Context usable;
7. discovery/option preparation allowed;
8. Booking Request creation allowed;
9. attribution possible for a qualifying activity.

An actor can reach one outcome without the next. Request creation does not imply Booking, earnings or Stay operations. Eligibility or relationship revocation changes future outcomes under policy and preserves legitimate historical actions.
