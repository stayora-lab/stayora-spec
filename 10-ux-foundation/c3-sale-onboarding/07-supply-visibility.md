# Supply visibility boundary

Sale visibility is a scoped projection of public marketplace truth plus any Sale-context information permitted by eligibility, Distribution Relationship, Property Distribution Policy, resource scope and privacy policy.

| Information / outcome | C3 boundary |
|---|---|
| Public/Guest listing, trust signals, Public Price | May be discoverable where marketplace/public policy permits |
| Availability | Derived Inventory truth; Sale can consume it, not mutate it |
| Bookability | Separate from Availability and subject to Booking/transaction conditions |
| Sale-context terms | Only where canonical relationship/policy grants them; otherwise TBD |
| Owner/Host private data and economics | Need-to-know and separate authority; not exposed because actor is Sale |

`Searchability ≠ Availability ≠ Bookability`. Eligibility or a relationship does not make every Property visible, available or bookable. C3 does not design the Sale workspace or a ranking/dispatch system.
