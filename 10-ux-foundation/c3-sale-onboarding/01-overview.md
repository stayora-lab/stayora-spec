# C3 Overview

**Purpose.** Apply the C1 onboarding chain to the Sale participant: `Identity → Sale capacity/relationship → platform eligibility → Distribution Relationship → resource/market scope → Sale Working Context → bounded distribution action → attribution where canonical`.

**Known truth.** CP3 says Sale requires platform eligibility and a Sale↔Commercial Authority relationship for distribution work. CP5/B1 says Sale may discover supply, prepare/share options and create a Booking Request; Host or delegated Booking Authority decides. CP4/CP7 keep eligibility, relationship, authority, attribution and money separate.

**C3 does not decide.** It does not create a universal Sale state, choose application criteria, invent partner contracts, choose attribution winners, expose Owner economics, define commission percentages, create Inventory/Booking/Stay authority, or design screens and technical onboarding.

**Readiness target.** An Identity may have Sale capacity recognized but remain ineligible, eligible but unlinked to a Distribution Relationship, linked but resource-scoped, or context-usable without every downstream action being available. These are independent outcomes.
