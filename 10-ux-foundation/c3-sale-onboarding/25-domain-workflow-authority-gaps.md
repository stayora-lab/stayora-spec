# Domain, workflow and authority gaps

| Class | Observed need | Existing concept | Why insufficient / escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| POLICY GAP | Sale approval and eligibility criteria | Application + Platform Eligibility | Criteria/decision procedure are not canonical; Founder/Product Architect |
| AUTHORITY GAP | Who establishes Distribution Relationship and exact scope | Distribution Authority + Actor Relationship | Grantor/contract/scope policy required |
| WORKFLOW GAP | Multi-Sale attribution conflict and replacement | Attribution/history | Qualification and appeal workflow required |
| PRIVACY / PRESENTATION NEED | Sale-facing commercial/private information | Scoped visibility and CP6 Sale Workspace | Field-level policy is open; do not infer |
| DOMAIN GAP | Offer/attribution ownership at qualification boundaries | Conceptual Offer, Lead, Request, Booking | CP4 leaves ownership/lifecycle boundaries open |
| WORKFLOW GAP | Eligibility/revocation mid-Request | Eligibility + Request lifecycle | Revalidation/notification policy required |
| AUTHORITY GAP | Sale report versus authoritative External Accommodation | Record External Commitment capability | Grant/evidence policy required |
| V0-SCOPE GAP | Manual Sale approval and relationship operations | CP5 manual assistance | Confirm pilot operating procedure without expanding scope |

C3 adds no entity, aggregate, state, permission or partner contract to close these gaps.
