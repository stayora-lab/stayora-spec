# Butler Context Convergence

**Primary destination:** `BUT-01 Today & Assignments`.

`BUT-02 Stay / Assignment Detail` is contextual to an assigned Stay. Readiness evidence is embedded in that responsibility. `BUT-03 Arrival / Check-in Entry` and `BUT-05 Checkout Entry` are action flow placeholders. `BUT-04 Incident Entry & Detail` is a contextual attention/detail entry from Today or assigned Stay.

This supports field-critical use without creating a workforce/task-management suite or making every operational step a navigation destination.
