# Booking ↔ Stay Stress Test

Booking and Stay remain separate canonical objects and lifecycles. Host may have a Bookings & Stays responsibility group with separate contextual collections/details. Guest enters primarily through Stay Hub. Operations enters directly through Stay/Assignment contexts. Sale follows Request/Booking outcome and attribution, not the full operational Stay. External Accommodation may establish a Stay without a Stayora Booking. No structural composition changes these semantics.
