# Money and Privacy Boundaries

No automatic Finance, Wallet, Earnings, Payout or Commission destination is added. `HST-12`, `SAL-05` and `ADM-05` remain contextual projections. Payment ≠ Entitlement ≠ Settlement ≠ Payout; Attribution ≠ Commission Entitlement.

Composition does not widen visibility: Guest data stays Stay-scoped; Owner economics remain relationship/authority scoped; Sale sees permitted commercial information; Butler sees assigned operational information; BQL sees destination/function information; Admin sees staff-function/exception information. Shared structural containers never imply shared data access. Exact field, QR, contact and retention rules remain TBD.
