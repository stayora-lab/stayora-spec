# Stayora Admin Convergence

**Primary destinations:** `ADM-01 Applications & Eligibility` as Governance/Attention and `ADM-06 Audit & Provenance` as a distinct governance inspection responsibility where durable audit work requires entry.

`ADM-02 Property & Verification`, `ADM-03 Inventory & External Exceptions`, `ADM-04 Incident Governance` and `ADM-05 Money Exceptions` are contextual governance/exception collections reached from ADM-01, attention or a governed object. They are not CRUD sections for every domain table.

Admin remains function/resource scoped, manual-assist and auditable; it is not a universal super-user or policy bypass.
