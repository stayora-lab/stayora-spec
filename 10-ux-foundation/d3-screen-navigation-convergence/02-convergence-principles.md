# Convergence Principles

1. One canonical truth with contextual projections.
2. Identity, Working Context and Authority remain distinct.
3. Navigation follows durable responsibility, not database tables or role labels.
4. Every D2 concept receives an explicit disposition; no concept disappears silently.
5. Primary destinations require repeated or responsibility-critical work, a meaningful collection/overview and independent entry intent.
6. Object details are reached from the responsible destination; they do not become global navigation by default.
7. Actions and action flows are entries from object context, not destinations unless a durable responsibility requires a collection.
8. Structural composition may share containers while preserving Request/Booking/Stay, Property/Unit, Incident/Maintenance Block and Money distinctions.
9. Context or resource switching changes projection and scope, never permission or authority.
10. Attention is a projection of domain facts, not a generic Task domain or priority algorithm.
11. Guest access, Sale, Butler, BQL and Admin remain scoped according to D1/D2.
