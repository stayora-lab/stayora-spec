# Operations — Butler Context: Task Architecture

**Surface:** Operations—Butler Context · **V0:** MUST BUILD assigned Stay operations; field/mobile priority.

| ID | Task | Type | Structural home | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BUT-T1 | Understand today's assigned operational responsibility | Primary | `BUT-01 Today & Assignments` | Active eligibility + Butler Assignment |
| BUT-T2 | Prepare Property/Unit and record readiness evidence | Primary | `BUT-02 Stay/Assignment Detail` | Readiness evidence; not Inventory authority |
| BUT-T3 | Support arrival and perform authorized Check-in | Primary | `BUT-03 Arrival / Check-in Entry` | Only if authorized by CP3/C4 |
| BUT-T4 | Support Guest during Stay | Primary | `BUT-02` | Need-to-know Guest/Stay data |
| BUT-T5 | Record Incident/evidence and escalate | Primary/attention | `BUT-04 Incident Entry & Detail` | Incident ≠ Maintenance Block; escalation handoff |
| BUT-T6 | Support authorized Checkout and completion evidence | Primary | `BUT-05 Checkout Entry` | No settlement decision |
| BUT-T7 | Understand escalation result where relevant | Supporting | `BUT-04` / `BUT-01` | Outcome projection only |

No workforce-management suite or Butler Inventory/Booking/commercial controls are created.
