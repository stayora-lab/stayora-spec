# Money Boundary

Money-related concepts appear only where V0 tasks require them. Payment, Entitlement, Settlement and Payout remain separate. Required Payment Condition is not every future Payment Obligation; Payment `UNKNOWN` is unresolved truth, not an invented failure state. Sale Attribution is not Commission Entitlement and neither is Settlement/Payout.

- Guest: own payment requirement/transaction projection where canonical.
- Host/Owner: authorized commercial and reconciliation truth.
- Sale: permitted attribution/outcome and any canonical earnings projection; no invented commission rate or dashboard.
- Butler/BQL: normally no unrelated economics.
- Admin: functionally required exception/provenance view.

Collection model, commission economics, tax and legal validation remain upstream policy/TBD. D2 does not settle them.
