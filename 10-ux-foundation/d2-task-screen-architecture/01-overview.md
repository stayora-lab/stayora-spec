# D2 Overview

## Purpose

D2 gives every V0 primary task a structural UX home while preserving domain truth, authority and cross-context handoffs from D1. A screen is a responsibility projection, not a table, role dashboard or permission bundle.

## Acceptance boundary

The output is a reviewable inventory of conceptual views and task paths. It is not a final sitemap, route map, visual hierarchy, wireframe, component specification or interaction design.

## V0 discipline

The inventory is derived only from CP5 MUST BUILD or MANUAL-ASSISTED journeys and accepted CP8 B1–B5/C1–C4 outcomes. Deferred suites (PMS, channel manager, Managed Operations, advanced CRM/affiliate/lead distribution, workforce/maintenance, universal reputation, dynamic pricing, native apps and enterprise IAM) have no screens here.

## Readiness

**READY WITH CONDITIONS** for Founder/Product Architect review. Conditions are documented policy, Owner perspective, privacy, attention ordering and detailed interaction gaps; no unresolved item is silently closed.
