# Navigation Inputs (Not Final Navigation)

D2 supplies responsibility groups only:

- Public: destinations, accommodation, date/intent, request entry.
- Guest: Stay, arrival/access, help, review.
- Host: responsibility/attention, properties, requests, bookings, stays, inventory interventions, operations, money/quality.
- Sale: supply discovery, options/requests, outcomes, permitted attribution.
- Butler: today/assignments, assigned stay, arrival/check-in, incidents, checkout.
- BQL: operations today, active stays, incidents, access/services.
- Admin: applications/eligibility, property/verification, inventory/external exceptions, incident governance, money exceptions, audit.

Context/resource switching is needed where one identity has multiple roles or multiple Properties/Destinations. No sidebar, bottom navigation, tabs, routes or final labels are selected.
