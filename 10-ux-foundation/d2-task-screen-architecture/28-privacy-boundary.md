# Privacy / Need-to-Know Boundary

Each concept classifies information broadly as PUBLIC, RELATIONSHIP-SCOPED, RESOURCE-SCOPED, OPERATIONAL NEED-TO-KNOW, COMMERCIAL NEED-TO-KNOW, ADMIN/EXCEPTION-SCOPED or PRIVATE/NOT VISIBLE. Exact field permissions, QR/link lifetime, consent, retention and contact disclosure remain policy/TBD.

Guest Stay Access is scoped; a QR/credential is not authority. Sale sees permitted commercial information and attribution only. Butler/BQL see operational data required for assigned/destination function. Admin sees only function/resource-scoped exceptions. Screen visibility cannot be used as a shortcut around CP3 authority or privacy boundaries.
