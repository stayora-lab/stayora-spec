# B4 — Butler Boundary

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

## Butler may do within valid assignment/capability

- Prepare an assigned villa/Stay for expected arrival.
- See assigned upcoming/current Stay, arrival, access and need-to-know Guest operational information.
- Coordinate arrival, Check-in and Checkout where the relevant capability is granted.
- Report operational readiness, access problems, damage/operational observations and Incidents.
- Support the Guest and coordinate destination services within scope.
- Preserve evidence, timestamps and actual acting capacity.

## Butler does not automatically gain

- Booking acceptance or rejection authority.
- Pricing, Offer or Inventory authority.
- Payment, refund, Settlement or Payout authority.
- Owner or Primary Host authority.
- Authority to assign blame or impose Reputation, Verification, financial or compensation consequences.
- Access to Public Price, Guest payment amount, Owner payout, Sale commission, Stayora fee or full settlement ledger merely because the Butler operates a Stay.

## See versus act

Butler may consume a scoped Stay projection without being allowed to change the Booking, commercial source, Inventory or financial truth. A readiness or Incident report is evidence/operational input; it is not a commercial mutation. Lack of Butler assignment does not erase Host operational responsibility.

Exact assignment propagation, replacement, access lifecycle, severity and escalation remain CP3/CP6 policy questions.
