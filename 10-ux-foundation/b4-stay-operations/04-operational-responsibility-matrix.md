## Capacity distinction

| Capacity | Meaning in B4 |
|---|---|
| Action owner | Responsible for the operational action within scope. |
| Information consumer | May see the projection needed for a function. |
| Evidence recorder | May record an observation or evidence; recording is not necessarily transition authority. |
| Exception owner | Receives unresolved work/case responsibility under policy. |
| Commercial authority | Booking, pricing, payment, settlement or Inventory authority; not implied by operations. |
| Operational authority | Stay/arrival/access/Incident action within assigned scope; not commercial authority. |

One Identity may hold several capacities; each action retains actual actor, acting capacity, authority basis, resource and time.
