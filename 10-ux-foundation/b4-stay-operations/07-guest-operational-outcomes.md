# B4 — Guest Operational Outcomes

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Moment | Guest outcome | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Upcoming Stay | Know the relevant accommodation, dates, party/access preparation and help path where eligible. | Does not prove Check-in or expose internal commerce. |
| Arrival approaching | Receive relevant instructions/status and know how to obtain support. | Does not create an Arrival/Check-in state automatically. |
| Physical arrival | Use the supported access/support path and report a problem. | Arrival/access does not equal formal Check-in. |
| Checked-in | Know that the Stay is operationally checked in and how to request help. | Does not confirm payment, Settlement or future completion. |
| In Stay | See own relevant details, access/help, destination information and issue status. | Does not expose other Guests, Owner economics or internal authority. |
| Incident | Report an issue and receive an appropriate acknowledgement/status where policy allows. | Does not determine blame, compensation, refund or Reputation. |
| Checkout | Understand departure requirements and whether Checkout is recorded. | Checkout does not automatically mean Completed or release Inventory. |
| Completed | Receive a truthful completion outcome and eligible follow-up/Review Right. | Completion does not automatically create Review, Reputation, Settlement or Payout. |

No Guest app, screen, chat, QR implementation, notification provider or authentication mechanism is designed here. These are product outcomes and boundary statements only.
