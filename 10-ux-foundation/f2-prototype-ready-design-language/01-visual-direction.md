# F2. Visual direction

## Accepted direction

**Status: ACCEPTED — F2 FOUNDER VISUAL DECISION, with four semantic presentation corrections.** The accepted direction is warm hospitality with operational precision:

- warm cream canvas and subtly differentiated light surfaces;
- warm dark foreground for calm readability;
- restrained purple brand/action direction;
- strong accommodation imagery on public surfaces, never as a substitute for truth;
- simple cards and list rows with clear hierarchy;
- tabular numerals for dates, amounts and operational counts;
- sans-first product language with a selective editorial/display accent;
- calm, functional motion.

The overall direction and listed reference values are accepted as the CP8-G baseline. Implementation-level mappings remain subject to accessibility and engineering review; CP8 semantics always outrank visual reference.

## Character by context

| Context | Visual character |
|---|---|
| Public Marketplace | Warm, spacious, image-supported and confidence-building. Public projection remains explicit about dates, price and bookability. |
| Guest Stay Access | Calm, legible and action-focused. Operational access is more important than promotional styling. |
| Host/Sale | Structured, scannable and moderately dense, with clear object identity and acting context. |
| Butler/Destination-BQL | Field-ready and information-dense, with strong attention/evidence cues and touch-safe actions. |
| Admin | Restrained, evidence-oriented and dense; elevation and color never imply super-authority. |

One system is expressed through shared tokens, type roles, outcome language and primitive anatomy. It does not require identical surfaces or density.

## Visual review guardrails

The accepted palette, typography, radius, shadow and composition values remain a design-language baseline, not a new brand decision. They do not define a domain state and cannot override CP8-A–E, F1 or an upstream TBD.
