- font licensing, fallback and localization; the Jakarta/Cormorant direction is accepted;
- exact primitive API, component names, package choices and token-file mapping;
- exact breakpoint, container, table/calendar and mobile-navigation behavior;
- dark mode/theme requirements;
- final radius, border, elevation and motion values;
- request expiry/withdrawal/amendment and offer validity;
- deposit/payment deadline/grace/refund/cancellation economics;
- Temporary Inventory Commitment duration and Emergency Protective Hold duration;
- Guest credential mechanism, security lifecycle, sharing, expiry and privacy;
- Admin/BQL grants, Sale economics, self-dealing policy and Attention lifecycle;
- external evidence standards and Inventory release consequences;
- any new actor, workspace or domain state.
