# B1 versus B3 — Sale-Assisted and Direct Guest Booking

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Dimension | B1 — Sale-assisted Booking | B3 — Direct Guest Booking |
|---|---|---|
| Demand initiator | Guest need reaches an eligible Sale context. | Guest enters Public Marketplace directly. |
| Discovery actor | Sale searches and presents supply. | Guest searches/evaluates public supply. |
| Request creator | Sale creates Request for the Guest demand context. | Guest creates Request, or an explicitly supported representative; Sale is not required. |
| Guest representation | Guest/staying-party information is supplied through Sale-assisted context. | Guest person/party is represented directly; account is not mandatory. |
| Sale attribution | Present when a legitimate Sale relationship/source exists. | Absent unless independently genuine; never fabricated. |
| Booking Authority | Host/Primary Host or delegated Co-host accepts/rejects. | Same. Guest and Sale cannot substitute for Host authority. |
| Inventory behavior | Request does not reserve; accepted Request revalidates and may create finite commitment. | Same. Removing Sale does not change Inventory truth. |
| Payment responsibility | Guest/Payer fulfils action-specific condition. | Same. Payer may differ from Guest/creator/staying party. |
| Guest communication | Sale may explain progress; Guest sees scoped projections. | Public/Guest contexts provide progress and confirmation directly, subject to access/privacy policy. |
| Booking confirmation | Booking begins at `CONFIRMED` after all conditions. | Same. Payment success alone is insufficient. |
| Stay handoff | Confirmed accommodation becomes operationally relevant. | Same. Booking remains separate from Stay. |
| Operations convergence | Host/Butler/BQL receive need-to-know operational truth. | Same. Operational visibility does not add commerce authority. |

## Architecture test

Removing Sale from B1 does not require changing the underlying Request, Inventory, Payment, Booking or Stay truth. The difference is the initiating context, discovery responsibility, Request creator and source attribution. If a future implementation requires a different Booking or Inventory model solely because the Guest arrived directly, that is an implementation or policy contradiction requiring review.

## Direct path is not automatic Instant Book

B3 preserves the Host/authorized Booking Authority decision. Instant Book can be evaluated as a separate controlled branch only if its eligibility, authority, commitment, payment timing and conflict behavior are explicitly decided upstream.
