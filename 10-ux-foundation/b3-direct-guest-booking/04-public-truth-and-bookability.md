# B3 — Public Truth, Searchability, Availability and Bookability

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

## Public truth needed for a Guest decision

The Public Marketplace may project only the canonical, Guest-appropriate truth needed to evaluate an accommodation choice:

| Public projection | Meaning in B3 | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Destination / Property / Bookable Unit | The accommodation context and unit the Guest may evaluate. | Public visibility does not grant commercial or operational authority. |
| Published content | Descriptive information accepted for public presentation. | It is not a promise that dates are Available or Bookable. |
| Verification status where eligible for display | Trust/quality signal from the Verification domain. | Identity verification/platform eligibility remains Identity & Authority; Stayora Verified is not a luxury claim. |
| Public Price / applicable Offer terms | Price or commercial terms available in the relevant Guest context. | Do not expose Owner economics, Sale economics, settlement or internal notes. |
| Searchability | Supply can participate in discovery. | Searchability is not Availability. |
| Availability | Derived Inventory truth for the requested range. | Availability is not actor/context Bookability. |
| Restrictions / conditions | Conditions material to the Guest action where canonical. | Do not invent occupancy, deposit, cancellation or payment rules. |

## Three separate tests

| Test | Definition | B3 consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Searchable | Supply is eligible to appear in discovery. | Guest may inspect it, but no action is implied. |
| Available | Derived Inventory truth indicates no effective blocking commitment for the relevant range, subject to canonical semantics. | Guest can evaluate a date range, but an authority/policy check may still be required. |
| Bookable | This Guest, context and action may legitimately proceed under applicable authority, eligibility and policy. | Only this test supports creation of the next authorized action, normally a Request. |

## Public evaluation outcomes

- **Searchable and Available:** Guest may proceed to evaluate and, where action conditions are met, create a Request.
- **Searchable but unavailable:** Guest may view the supply; the requested range cannot be represented as available. Do not create a reservation or Booking.
- **Available but not currently Bookable:** Preserve the Availability projection while explaining or routing the actor/policy limitation without inventing the reason.
- **Availability unknown or stale:** Surface uncertainty and require the next supported revalidation; do not promise the dates.
- **Availability changes before Request:** Re-evaluate before creating the Request; Guest intent alone creates no commitment.
- **Availability changes after Request:** The Request remains separate from Inventory and follows the existing conflict/revalidation policy; do not silently confirm or choose a winner.

## Guest-visible and private truth

Guest-facing projections may include the accommodation, dates, applicable public terms and material trust/availability signals. They do not automatically expose Owner economics, Sale commission, internal settlement, private operational notes, authority structures or conflict details. The exact disclosure and privacy boundary remains a policy/UX TBD where CP6 has not specified it.

For the same villa-night/context, B3 follows the canonical **One Public Price** principle. It does not restore a historical direct premium, direct Instant Book discount or hidden Sale spread; discount and funding policy remains separate and open where upstream documents leave it open.
