# B2 — B1 versus B2 Comparison

> Status: **DRAFT — FOUNDER / PRODUCT ARCHITECT REVIEW** · **NOT FROZEN**

| Dimension | B1 — Sale-assisted Booking | B2 — External Booking → Stay |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Sale-assisted demand progresses through Stayora commerce. | Commerce originates outside Stayora. |
| Request existence | Sale creates a Booking Request; Host/authorized Co-host decides. | No Stayora Booking Request is required for external accommodation. A report may exist, but it is not a fake Request. |
| Booking existence | Booking begins at `CONFIRMED` after confirmation conditions. | No Stayora Booking is created merely by recording external accommodation. |
| Commerce provenance | Stayora commercial path with Offer/terms, Request, confirmation and attribution where supported. | External source remains original commercial truth; source/provenance stays visible. |
| Inventory basis | Host-authorized decision may lead to Temporary Commitment, then Confirmed Accommodation Commitment. | Authoritative external fact may lead to an authorized external Inventory Commitment; fact and commitment remain distinct. |
| Payment truth | Required Payment Condition, Payment Obligation/Attempt and confirmation evaluation are relevant. | External payment remains external; no Stayora Payment/Obligation is fabricated merely for operations. |
| Sale attribution | Sale attribution may exist if B1/source policy supports it. | No automatic Sale attribution; external Sale/off-platform source remains provenance, not entitlement. |
| Stay creation/representation | Confirmed Booking can provide Accommodation Basis for Stay. | External Accommodation can provide Accommodation Basis for Stay without Stayora Booking. |
| Guest access | Scoped access after confirmed commerce/represented Stay; QR/privacy open. | Scoped access may be provided to external Guest under same relationship/access boundaries; origin remains external. |
| Butler/BQL relevance | Emerges after Booking creates operationally relevant Stay. | Emerges after authoritative external Stay representation becomes operationally relevant. |
| Historical truth | Booking, Stay, Money and attribution remain separate and auditable. | External source, fact, Inventory history, Stay, incident and corrections remain separate; no fake commerce history. |

## Shared model

Both journeys converge on shared Inventory and operational Stay truth without collapsing provenance. B1 starts from a Stayora Request/Booking path; B2 starts from an external fact/report path. Neither path lets operational visibility grant commercial authority.
