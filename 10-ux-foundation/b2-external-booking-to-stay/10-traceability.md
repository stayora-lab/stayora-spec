## V0 boundary

The matrix uses CP5 external Booking/Stay registration, external Confirmed Accommodation Commitment, conflict detection, Stay lifecycle, operational access and basic Audit Trail. It does not introduce Channel Manager, PMS, integrations, external accounting, automated reconciliation or any deferred subsystem.
