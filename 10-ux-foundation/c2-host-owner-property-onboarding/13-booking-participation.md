# Booking participation prerequisites

For Sale-assisted or Direct Guest participation, C2 only establishes upstream prerequisites; it does not redesign B1 or B3. A Unit must be represented, discoverable where the journey requires it, supported by current Inventory truth, and connected to an actor with explicit Booking Authority and any applicable commercial eligibility. A request remains a request until the canonical Booking workflow changes it; onboarding does not create a Booking.

`Searchable ≠ Available ≠ Bookable`. Inventory truth can change independently of Property publication and relationships. A Sale may search, prepare an Offer or create a Request under CP3 scope, but Sale eligibility does not become Booking or Inventory Authority. Direct Guest participation does not require the Owner and Host to be the same Identity.

Unresolved commercial, payment, cancellation, attribution and acceptance policies remain downstream TBDs. C2 records their dependency without resolving them.
