# Stay Operations prerequisites

The B2/B4-compatible prerequisite view is:

- Property and relevant Unit are represented;
- Destination context is established where operations require it;
- Guest/Stay truth can be associated, whether the origin is internal or external;
- a Host/operations responsibility or explicit assignment exists for the action;
- Butler assignment, if used, is established separately and within scope.

These conditions support an operational Stay context without making Booking the sole operational source of truth. External commerce can establish Inventory truth and/or a Stay; it need not create a Stayora Booking. Butler onboarding is not designed here. Incident, access, check-in/out and operational policy remain CP4/B4/C4 concerns.
