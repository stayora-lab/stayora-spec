# Entry modes, invitations and claims

The following modes are conceptual inputs, not technical mechanisms: self-entry, Owner invitation, Host invitation, Admin-assisted Property representation, Property-first setup, actor-first setup, existing Property association and existing Identity linkage.

Invitation is a proposal to an intended recipient. Acceptance may provide consent or participation, but does not by itself establish Owner, Host, authority or assignment. A claim is an assertion such as “I own this villa” or “I host this property”; it is not truth. Evidence is preserved as a basis for a later relationship decision and does not itself equal authority.

No evidence threshold, legal verification process, automatic matching rule, conflict winner or technical invitation protocol is defined here. Pending claims must remain visibly pending and must not unlock publication, Booking, Inventory or financial effects unless a separate canonical relationship/authority decision permits them.
