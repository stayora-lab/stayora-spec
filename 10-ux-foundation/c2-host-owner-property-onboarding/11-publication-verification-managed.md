# Publication, Verification and Managed boundary

`Property represented` is not `published`; `published/searchable` is not `Available` or `Bookable`; `Published` is not `Verified`; `Verified` is not `Managed`.

Publication may require the applicable Property authority, minimum Property/Unit representation, Inventory responsibility, public commercial information and other canonical eligibility conditions. C2 does not invent a moderation policy or make Verification universal. Stayora Verified is a supply-quality/trust program owned by Verification; Identity verification/platform eligibility remains Identity & Authority. Owner Listed, Stayora Verified and Stayora Managed remain distinct supply modes. Managed is outside current V0 core and is never the default onboarding path.

Where a Property is represented but not publishable, the projection should explain the unresolved condition without implying a new lifecycle state. A Property can remain useful for private, administrative or relationship work.
