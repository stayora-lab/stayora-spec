# Identity, Party, Owner and Host

Identity is the person/account capable of acting. Party is the business, legal or economic subject. Account is an interaction mechanism. Owner and Host are capacities/relationships, not alternate identities.

| Concept | C2 meaning | Explicit non-implication |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Ownership-related relationship to a Property/Party when established by valid basis | Not automatically Primary Host, Booking Authority, Inventory Authority or beneficiary |
| Primary Host | Hosting/commercial responsibility anchor for a Property when explicitly established | Not automatically Owner or financial beneficiary |
| Co-host | Explicit delegated hosting relationship and scope | Does not inherit all Host authority |
| Property creator | Actor who represents or edits a candidate resource under current authorization | Not automatically Owner, Host or any commercial authority |

One Identity may hold Owner and Primary Host capacities at once; the relationship records remain distinct and an action is attributed to the capacity and authority basis actually used. Different Identities may hold them; C2 does not invent precedence when the canonical record is silent.
