# Duplicate representation and claim conflict

Two people may claim one Property; one Property may be represented twice; Owner and Host may disagree; a Host relationship may be disputed; or the physical/business identity may be uncertain. C2 preserves:

- each claim and its provenance;
- existing authoritative relationships and grants;
- evidence/basis and timestamps;
- the conflict/exception boundary;
- the decision responsibility and unresolved status.

C2 does not choose a winner, invent ownership precedence, create a universal dispute system or grant temporary authority merely because a claim is first. Discovery, publication, Booking and Inventory consequences remain blocked or policy-governed until canonical truth is sufficient.
