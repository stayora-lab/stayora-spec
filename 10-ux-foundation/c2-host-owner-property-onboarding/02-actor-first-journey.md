# Actor-first journey

The actor-first path starts with an existing or new Identity and an intent to represent an Owner or Host capacity. It may end with a pending relationship, a represented Property, or a bounded context; it does not presume the actor owns or operates the resource.

| Step | Canonical effect | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Resolve Identity and Party/capacity | Identify the acting Identity and relevant Party/capacity | Account creation is not Owner/Host truth |
| A2 State intent or claim | Create a proposed Owner/Host relationship input | Claim is not relationship, evidence or authority |
| A3 Identify or represent Property | Establish a Property representation and candidate Destination/Unit links | Creator is not Owner, Host or authority |
| A4 Gather relationship basis | Preserve invitation, existing association, evidence or Admin-assisted basis | No invented threshold or legal conclusion |
| A5 Establish relationship if authorized | Record Owner, Primary Host or delegated Co-host relationship with scope/validity | Ownership does not imply Hosting/Booking/Inventory/finance |
| A6 Evaluate eligibility and authority | Apply existing platform eligibility and explicit capability grants | Role or Working Context does not grant permission |
| A7 Project bounded context | Show the actor only the resources and actions supported by current truth | Publication, Booking and Stay readiness are separate |

Pending outcomes are legitimate: Property represented with Owner unresolved; Owner established without Host; Host established without publication; or relationship awaiting policy/evidence decision. The exact acceptance policy remains a TBD where canonical sources are silent.
