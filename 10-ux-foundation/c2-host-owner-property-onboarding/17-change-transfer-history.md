# Relationship change, transfer and historical truth

Owner correction/change, Primary Host replacement, Co-host revocation, Unit correction, Destination correction where permitted and duplicate consolidation are modeled as relationship/resource corrections or supersession with provenance. A revoked or replaced relationship affects future authority and context according to policy; it does not silently rewrite prior valid Booking, Inventory or Stay actions.

Transfer and legal ownership processes are not designed here. The record should retain who acted, under what capacity/basis, on which resource, at what time, and what later correction superseded. The exact effective-time, notice, appeal and downstream policy is `TBD / FOUNDER DECISION` where not established by CP4/CP7.
