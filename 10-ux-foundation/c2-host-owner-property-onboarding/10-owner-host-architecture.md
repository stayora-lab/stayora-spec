# Owner and Host relationship journeys

## Owner relationship

Self-claim, invitation, existing Property association and Admin-assisted representation are possible bases. The conceptual sequence is `claim/intent → basis/evidence → relationship establishment → authority effects`. A statement of ownership never creates authoritative ownership truth automatically. Multiple Owners, transfer and dispute handling remain subject to canonical legal/product decisions; C2 preserves them as alternatives and does not choose a threshold or winner.

## Host relationship

The Host relationship may be established when an Owner designates a Host, an existing Host is invited/accepted, or an authorized setup establishes a Primary Host. An Owner may also be Primary Host, but the two relationships remain distinct. Co-host is later delegated explicitly and scoped; it is not a synonym for Primary Host.

## Effects

Established relationships may support explicit Property, Booking, Inventory or Operations capabilities only when the relevant capability grant and scope exist. They do not automatically change publication, Verification, Managed, financial entitlement or historical actions. Relationship validity, grantor, scope and revocation are recorded conceptually; exact policies remain TBD where absent.
