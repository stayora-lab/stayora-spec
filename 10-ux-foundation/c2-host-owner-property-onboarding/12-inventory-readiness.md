# Inventory responsibility and readiness

Inventory truth is Unit × Time. Before participation, the system needs a represented Unit, an identified Inventory responsibility basis and an actor with explicit Inventory Authority for the relevant action and scope. Ownership or Host role alone does not supply unrestricted Inventory Authority.

The authorized action may be an Owner Block, Maintenance/Unusable Block, Temporary Exclusive Commitment, Confirmed Accommodation Commitment or an External Accommodation-backed commitment, using the B5/B2 boundaries. Sale eligibility, whitelist or demand access alone cannot record an external accommodation or establish a commitment.

External commerce remains an external concept: it may be referenced by Inventory and/or create a Stay; a Stayora Booking is not required. C2 does not design a calendar, Availability control or conflict policy. Overlap, correction, release and precedence remain governed by CP4/B5 and any explicit policy TBD.
