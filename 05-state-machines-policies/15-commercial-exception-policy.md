# Commercial Exception Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Keep CHANGE, CANCELLATION, PAYMENT DEFAULT, and NO-SHOW/NO-ARRIVAL separate. Change is an authorized replacement/supersession process requiring authority, inventory revalidation and applicable commercial terms. Where an existing accommodation commitment must be replaced, the valid original commitment remains protected until the replacement is safely established according to policy; if replacement fails, the original right is not silently destroyed. A terminal commitment does not silently revive. Cancellation is authorized intentional termination; validity does not determine refund. Payment Default is a policy determination and may terminate the accommodation commitment only after applicable conditions. No-show is operational non-use and does not automatically terminate a valid right or release Inventory.

While the right remains valid, the Guest may arrive late. Full-period non-use may later produce a no-use/no-show disposition. Never fake Stay COMPLETED for settlement. Exception Economic Eligibility may exist without Completed. Exact cancellation economics, refund, forfeiture, and Payment Default consequences remain **TBD**.
