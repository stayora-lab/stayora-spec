# Review Rights model

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Review Rights are an eligibility/entitlement model, not a core state machine:

```text
Verified Interaction Evidence → Review Eligibility → Review Right
```

Normal basis: `Stay COMPLETED` may support Review Eligibility, subject to sufficient verified interaction evidence and applicable policy. External path: sufficient verified Stay evidence plus qualifying completion → Review Eligibility → Review Right. Commerce source does not determine review eligibility; sufficiently verified interaction does. Review Right ≠ Review. A future Review entity may have its own lifecycle, but this checkpoint does not define it. Exact evidence threshold and eligibility rules remain TBD.
