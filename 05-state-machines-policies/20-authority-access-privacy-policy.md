# Authority, Access and Privacy Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Identity ≠ Role ≠ Relationship ≠ Authority ≠ Permission ≠ Access/Visibility ≠ Credential ≠ Audit.

Effective Permission derives from Identity, Platform Eligibility, Actor Relationship, Authority Capability, Resource Scope, Authority Lifecycle, Resource/Destination Policy, and Transaction Context. Effective Access also considers Data Purpose and Lifecycle Context. Role alone is insufficient.

Operational Authority does not imply commercial/financial visibility. Sale may see appropriate Inventory, Guest-facing price, own commission, listing/trust data, and Booking status, but not automatically Owner payout/tax/full ledger. Butler/BQL operational access does not imply commercial visibility.

Guest disclosure is progressive by relationship, purpose, lifecycle, and authority. Destination/BQL receives need-to-know operations data, not Booking price, Sale commission, Owner payout, Affiliate attribution, or Guest payment history unless explicit policy requires it. QR/link/credential conveys only narrow scoped capability and supports scope, lifecycle, and revocation; it is not business Authority.

Delegation is explicit in capability, resource scope, and lifecycle; it cannot exceed the grantor, and re-delegation is not default. Revocation ends future access but not audit history. Authority is evaluated at action time: later revocation does not retroactively invalidate a legitimately authorized action. Staff administration does not imply impersonation; break-glass access is bounded, attributable, reasoned, time-limited where appropriate, and auditable.
