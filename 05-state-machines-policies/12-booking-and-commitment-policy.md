# Booking and Commitment Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Offer ≠ Booking Request ≠ Authorization to Commit ≠ Commercial Accommodation Commitment ≠ Booking.

Booking Confirmation Conditions are: valid Offer/Terms, valid Authority, available inventory exclusivity, applicable Required Payment Condition, explicit commercial consent, and other required compliance. They establish a Commercial Accommodation Commitment and Booking CONFIRMED. Required Payment Condition may be less than 100%.

Accepted Request creates finite authorization and may create a Temporary Exclusive Inventory Commitment plus a separate Payment Session/Attempt. If conditions succeed, the temporary commitment converts/ends into a Confirmed Accommodation Commitment and Booking becomes CONFIRMED. If conditions fail/expire, authorization/temporary commitment ends and Availability is recomputed. Do not create Cancelled Booking for a Booking that never existed.

Instant Book uses Host pre-authorization via policy, then converges at the same commitment boundary. Confirmed Booking creates a protected accommodation right. Late/no arrival/no Check-in does not terminate it; Payment Default Policy determines payment consequences. Exact commitment window remains **TBD**.
