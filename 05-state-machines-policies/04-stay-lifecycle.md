# Stay lifecycle

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

```text
SCHEDULED → CHECKED_IN → CHECKED_OUT → COMPLETED
SCHEDULED → DID_NOT_OCCUR
```

`DID_NOT_OCCUR` is a pre-Check-in/final-policy outcome when the accommodation commitment legitimately ends without a Stay occurring. `PREPARATION` and `READY` are operational readiness milestones, not core Stay states. `ARRIVAL` is an event/observation; `IN_STAY` is unnecessary.

Checkout ≠ Completed. Operational Completion Readiness belongs to Stay. Late arrival, no arrival, or missing Check-in while a valid accommodation right remains does not cancel Booking, create Payment Default, release Inventory, or create Completed. A final no-use disposition may be recorded after the whole period ends. Exact state/disposition representation remains **TBD**.
