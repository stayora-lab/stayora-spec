# Verification and Reputation Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

```text
SIGNAL → Qualification/Triage → Review Case (if warranted)
→ Evidence + Assessment → Finding/Decision
→ Verification / Reputation / Platform consequence
```

Signal ≠ Evidence ≠ Finding ≠ Responsibility ≠ Consequence. A low rating or complaint does not automatically suspend/remove Verified. A Review Case may be OPEN while Verification remains VERIFIED; serious qualifying risk may permit precautionary Suspension under explicit Policy. Suspension and Removal are different, and neither automatically delists a marketplace property.

Verification evaluates the property promise Stayora is willing to make; Reputation describes accumulated attributable experience/behavior. Reputation may inform Guest decisions, Owner relationships, Lead distribution, Verification review, and Platform Eligibility review, but does not acquire authority. Responsibility attribution precedes actor-specific negative consequences where fault matters. Disputed allegations are not established public facts. Weighting, decay, samples, appeals, visibility, and public algorithms remain **TBD**.
