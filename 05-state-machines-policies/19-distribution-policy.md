# Distribution Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Effective Distribution Capability derives from Sale Platform Eligibility + Sale↔Commercial Authority relationship + Property Distribution Policy + Transaction Context + Lead/Attribution Context. Sale Platform Eligibility does not grant Inventory Authority.

Relationship states are NORMAL, WHITELIST, and BLACKLIST. NORMAL allows discovery/compare/quote/request subject to policy. WHITELIST may unlock Instant Book only when all other conditions pass; it is not unrestricted permission. BLACKLIST is relationship-scoped and does not suspend Sale globally, affect unrelated owners, or erase history. Relationship is conceptually Sale ↔ Commercial Authority holder ↔ Authority Scope, not Property-scoped by default. Platform Suspension overrides Whitelist for the affected eligibility/capability; it does not silently suspend the Identity's unrelated roles or capabilities. Distribution Relationship has scope, lifecycle, provenance and audit history.

Lead acceptance creates demand responsibility, not supply authority. Do not finalize dispatch algorithm or SLA. Verified Sale facts may include Requests, commitment windows, Payment Defaults, Bookings, cancellations, Lead outcomes, completed Stays, and disputes; bad outcomes are not automatically misconduct. Affiliate ≠ Sale. A transaction may preserve separate Acquisition Attribution (Affiliate A), Sales Handling (Sale B), Commercial Authority (Host C), Property and Operations attribution. Sale assistance, replacement or remediation does not automatically erase valid acquisition attribution. Affiliate attribution does not create Inventory or Booking Authority; exact rate/window/qualification/fraud rules and onboarding approval remain **TBD**.
