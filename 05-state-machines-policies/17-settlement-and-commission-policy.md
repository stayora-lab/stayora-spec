# Settlement and Commission Policy

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Economic waterfall:

```text
Public Price / Booking Value → Discount Funding → Guest Booking Price
→ Commissionable Booking Value → Entitlements → Refunds/Adjustments
→ Final Economic Position → Settlement → Payout
```

This is conceptual economic architecture, not journal-entry order. Guest Total Payment ≠ Booking Value ≠ Commissionable Booking Value ≠ Stayora Revenue ≠ Owner Entitlement ≠ Payout. One Public Price and Sale base commission of 10% of Commissionable Booking Value are confirmed. Affiliate rate, platform fee and performance incentive remain WORKING MODEL/TBD.

Commissionable Booking Value is policy-determined eligible accommodation value, not total cash, final payment, or checkout total. Security/Damage Deposit is excluded; add-ons do not automatically belong. Every discount/adjustment has economic attribution and effect flags; no anonymous/unfunded discount. Conceptually preserve amount, funding party, reason/type, guest-price effect, CBV effect, Owner-entitlement effect and authorization/provenance. Sale-funded discounts normally reduce Sale entitlement but do not automatically reduce Owner entitlement or CBV. Owner-funded adjustments require valid Commercial Authority and do not automatically change CBV. Stayora-funded discounts affect Stayora's position and do not automatically reduce unrelated Owner/Sale entitlement. Mixed funding remains possible. Refunds/adjustments do not rewrite original CBV or successful payment.

Forfeiture is not automatically Owner Revenue or Commission Earned. Cancellation is not Refund; a Refund does not rewrite the original successful Payment. The refund executor may differ from the economic bearer. Supplier-caused failure must not mechanically use Guest-cancellation economics. Guest remediation may precede final responsibility allocation. Economic consequences derive from the applicable policy snapshot, reason, responsibility, verified facts and authorized adjustments.

Commission Amount ≠ Attribution ≠ Earning ≠ Payout. Expected entitlement may be PENDING, then EARNED or NOT_EARNED. Normal earning follows Completed and Economic Eligibility; exception eligibility may apply to a fully paid/no-use commitment. Payment Default and deposit forfeiture do not automatically earn commission. Later correction, clawback, error or fraud creates an adjustment/reversal event; it does not move EARNED back to NOT_EARNED or delete earned history. Supplier-failure economics must consider attributable commercial outcome and responsibility. Exact exception percentages remain **TBD**.
