# Role Application and Platform Eligibility

> Status: **REOPENED FOR RECONCILIATION — 2026-09-19**

Identity ≠ Role Application ≠ Platform Eligibility.

Generic Application:

```text
SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED
                       ↘ REJECTED
                       ↘ WITHDRAWN
```

APPROVED is a historical application outcome. Platform Eligibility is a status model:

```text
ELIGIBLE ↔ SUSPENDED → REVOKED
```

Reinstatement after revoke requires explicit auditable process/new application as applicable. Sale Approval Policy and Butler Approval Policy are separate. Sale Eligibility/ELIGIBLE is the canonical platform terminology; older CP3 `ACTIVE` wording maps to an effective eligible capability and is not a separate additional state. Sale Eligibility ≠ Sale–Host Relationship; Butler Eligibility ≠ Butler Assignment. One Identity may hold independent role eligibilities, and one capability suspension does not automatically suspend every role.
