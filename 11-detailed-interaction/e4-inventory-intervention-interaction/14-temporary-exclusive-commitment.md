# Temporary Exclusive Commitment

A Request is an intent, not an Inventory reservation or commitment. Where accepted policy permits, an authorized acceptance flow may establish a Temporary Exclusive Commitment after current truth and authority are revalidated.

Duration, hold release, extension, payment deadline, auto-cancel, priority and conflict precedence remain policy TBD. The interaction must not imply these values.
