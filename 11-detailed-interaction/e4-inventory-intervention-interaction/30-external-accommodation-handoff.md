# External Accommodation Handoff

External Commerce may flow to Inventory Truth and/or Stay. It does not route through Stayora Booking merely for modelling convenience. External Booking is an external concept referenced by Inventory and Stay; a Stayora Booking is optional.

The handoff distinguishes report, fact, evaluated commitment and operational Stay, with source/provenance and conflict posture. No external channel manager or automatic reconciliation is added.
