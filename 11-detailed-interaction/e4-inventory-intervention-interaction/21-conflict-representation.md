# Conflict Representation

A conflict projection identifies the overlapping resource/time, each competing truth or evidence source, actor/capacity, provenance, freshness posture, affected requests/bookings/stays and responsible reconciliation context. It distinguishes conflict from unavailable, blocked, unknown and empty.

Private reasons, Guest identity and commercial detail remain need-to-know; Public and Sale projections receive only their allowed derived outcome.
