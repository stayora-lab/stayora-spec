# Maintenance Block

A Maintenance Block is a distinct authorized Inventory decision with its own basis. `Incident ≠ Maintenance Block`; evidence or repair observation creates attention and possible handoff, not a block automatically.

E4 does not create a maintenance suite, vendor flow, SLA, repair lifecycle or duration rule. Any block is scoped, attributable and revalidated like other consequential Inventory interventions.
