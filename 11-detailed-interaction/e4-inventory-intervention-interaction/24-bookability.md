# Bookability

Bookability is contextual. It combines derived Availability with actor/source, distribution, policy and commercial context. Searchability is a separate discovery projection.

A Unit can be discoverable without being bookable, available without being bookable for a particular actor/channel, or bookable only after a contextual revalidation. Removing a block never guarantees universal Bookability.
