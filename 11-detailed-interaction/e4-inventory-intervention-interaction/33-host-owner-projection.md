# Host / Owner Projection

Host and Owner receive richer Unit × Time truth where their effective relationship and authority permit it: commitments, blocks, evidence, conflict, attention and provenance. Field visibility still follows need-to-know.

Projection richness never creates a grant. Distinct Owner and Host contexts remain visible where their authority differs.
