# Public Projection

Public surfaces may receive derived Availability, contextual Bookability and permitted trust/quality signals. They do not receive private block reasons, Guest identity, Owner intent, Incident evidence, internal commercial conflict or authority history.

Public projection is refreshed from canonical truth; it cannot create or mutate Inventory.
