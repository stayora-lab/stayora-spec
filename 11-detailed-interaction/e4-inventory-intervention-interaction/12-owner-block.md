# Owner Block

An Owner Block requires a legitimate initiator, effective Inventory authority, Unit × Time, reason/basis and any required evidence. Before commit, the interaction shows revalidation and overlapping commitments/blocks. The outcome includes canonical truth, conflict posture, projections, provenance and downstream attention.

E4 does not decide duration, priority, override, conflict winner or commercial consequence. Block removal is a separate authorized action and does not guarantee Bookability.
