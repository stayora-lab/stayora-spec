# Operations Projection

Butler and BQL receive operational impact, evidence and responsible attention within assignment/destination scope. They do not receive full Inventory controls or private commercial truth by default.

Operations projection points to authorized evaluation and handoff; it does not create a Block, Commitment or Availability change.
