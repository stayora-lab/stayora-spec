# Admin Interaction

Admin interaction is scoped governance and manual reconciliation. It may inspect conflict/provenance and route or record an authorized correction where the business authority is explicit. Admin is not a universal Inventory CRUD or super-admin override.

Admin assistance records requester, acting staff capacity, resource, reason, action, time, source truth and resulting outcome. It does not hide the responsible Owner/Host or invent a conflict winner.
