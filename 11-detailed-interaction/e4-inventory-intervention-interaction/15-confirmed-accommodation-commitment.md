# Confirmed Accommodation Commitment

A Booking or other canonical confirmed accommodation basis may establish a Confirmed Accommodation Commitment for the scoped Unit × Time. It remains distinct from Booking, Stay, Payment and Availability.

Confirmation consumes current Inventory truth and may surface conflict or manual reconciliation. It does not mean the Unit is universally bookable or that settlement, completion or release has occurred.
