# Unit × Time Scope

Every intervention names the affected Unit or resource and effective time range before authority is evaluated. Property is not Unit. A property-wide action is only available where an upstream rule defines its meaning; E4 does not invent inheritance, fan-out or overlap semantics.

Missing, ambiguous or changed scope blocks consequential action and routes to correction or manual reconciliation. The interaction must show the scope used for revalidation and provenance.
