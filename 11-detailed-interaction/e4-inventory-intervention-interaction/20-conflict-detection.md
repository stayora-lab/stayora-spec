# Conflict Detection

Conflict detection compares scoped Unit × Time truth and records overlap among blocks, temporary or confirmed commitments, external-backed commitments and corrections. It also detects stale facts and incompatible concurrent interventions.

Detection does not choose a winner. The interaction preserves all relevant facts, evidence, actors, provenance and downstream impact.
