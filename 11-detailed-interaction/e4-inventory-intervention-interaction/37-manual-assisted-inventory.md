# Manual-Assisted Inventory

V0 supports manual assistance where canonical truth or evidence is known but a human with the needed authority must evaluate or reconcile it: surface context → identify responsible capacity → perform or record authorized intervention → refresh projections → update originating attention.

Manual assistance is attributable and bounded. It does not become a generic operations workforce, maintenance suite or Admin override.
