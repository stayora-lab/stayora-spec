# Inventory Projection

Projections are audience-scoped views of canonical Inventory truth. They may show derived Availability, contextual Bookability, evidence posture, conflict, stale data or attention. They do not become a second Inventory source of truth.

Each projection identifies its basis, last known posture and next legitimate action where relevant. A projection refresh does not itself create, release or correct Inventory.
