# Interaction Sequences

The required sequences are covered: Host inspect; Owner Block; Owner Block conflict; operational evidence; Maintenance Block; Request acceptance→Temporary Commitment; Booking confirmation→confirmed commitment; External→evaluation; external-backed commitment; conflicting external; correction/supersession; release/removal; concurrent; stale; manual reconciliation; Availability refresh; Public/Sale refresh.

Every consequential sequence includes current truth, authority, Unit × Time, revalidation, outcome, projection, provenance and attention.

The sequence family also includes serious operational evidence → authorized Emergency Protective Hold → prevent new conflicting commitments → immediate escalation → authorized Maintenance Block/release/expiry outcome. Unresolved policy ends in a bounded branch.
