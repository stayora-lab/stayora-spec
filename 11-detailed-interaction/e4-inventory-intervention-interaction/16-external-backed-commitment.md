# External-backed Commitment

External Commerce is created outside Stayora. Stayora may reference or minimally record an External Booking to establish Inventory Truth and/or an operational Stay. A Stayora Booking is not required.

An External Accommodation Fact can support evaluation; it is not automatically a Commitment. After authorized evaluation, an External-backed Commitment may be represented with source, provenance and scope. `External Report ≠ External Fact`; `External Fact ≠ Commitment`.
