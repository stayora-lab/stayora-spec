# Calendar Boundary

A calendar cell is a visual Unit × Time projection. It is not an Inventory record, commitment or block. Drag, click, color or bulk selection cannot bypass authority, basis, revalidation or conflict handling.

E4 does not design a calendar UI, daily availability rows, editable Availability booleans, or a precedence algorithm. Any future calendar must call the same canonical intervention contract as another entry path.
