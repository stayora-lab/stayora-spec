# Incident Handoff

An Incident or operational evidence handoff contains source context, resource, time, evidence, reporter, responsible Inventory context, authority posture and resulting attention. The reporter can remain responsible for evidence without gaining Inventory intervention authority.

Only an authorized evaluation may establish a Maintenance Block or other Inventory truth. Commercial, reputation and settlement consequences remain separate.
