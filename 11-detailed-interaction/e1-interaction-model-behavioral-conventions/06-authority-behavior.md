# Authority Behavior

Interaction distinguishes **CAN SEE**, **CAN INITIATE**, **CAN ACT**, **CAN ACT IF AUTHORIZED**, **CANNOT ACT** and **AUTHORITY UNKNOWN / REQUIRES RESOLUTION**. Screen presence is never proof of permission.

If authority is missing, expired, outside resource scope, revoked, pending or changed while viewing: retain safe visibility where canonical, explain the reason category where safe, remove/disable the action behaviorally, revalidate on retry and route to the responsible context/manual assistance where applicable. Do not invent RBAC or disclose sensitive authority details.
