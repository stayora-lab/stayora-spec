# Interaction Principles

1. Expose canonical truth through a contextual projection; never make UI state authoritative.
2. Make acting context, resource scope and authority legible before consequential action.
3. Revalidate truth at the action boundary whenever concurrent change could alter the consequence.
4. Treat success, failure, conflict, stale truth, pending, processing, unknown and manual follow-up as distinct interaction outcomes.
5. Never claim consequential success before authoritative truth exists.
6. Preserve provenance and history for authority-sensitive, corrective, financial, inventory, external, incident and relationship actions.
7. Handoffs transfer responsibility or attention, not authority.
8. Keep Request, Booking, Stay, External Accommodation, Inventory, Incident, Money and attribution semantics distinct.
9. Do not encourage unsafe duplicate actions after uncertain outcomes.
10. Manual assistance records known truth and responsibility; it does not disguise work as automation.
11. Context/resource switching changes projection and scope, never permission.
12. Interaction conventions do not create lifecycle states or business policy.
