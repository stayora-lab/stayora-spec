# Request Decision Authority

Host/Primary Host/explicitly delegated Co-host may accept or reject only with valid Booking Authority, resource scope and current relationship. Sale may create a Request but cannot decide. Guest cannot decide. Butler/BQL cannot decide from operational capacity. A dual-capacity Identity must act under the actual Host/Co-host capacity; Sale attribution remains separate. Authority missing, revoked, expired or unknown yields NOT PERMITTED/AUTHORITY UNKNOWN, not a guessed decision.
