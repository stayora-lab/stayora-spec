# Pilot Metrics

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 5 DOCUMENTED; NOT FOUNDER FREEZE**

## North Star

**Destination Stay Coverage** = represented actual stays in Stayora / total actual stays within participating pilot scope. Represented stays may originate from Stayora, Airbnb, Booking.com, Zalo/Sale, Owner Direct or another evidenced source. Existing target `≥70%` is a **HYPOTHESIS**, not a hard threshold.

## Inventory Trust

Inventory Coverage, Inventory Accuracy, Inventory Conflict Rate and Manual Availability Recheck Rate. Existing `≥95%` Inventory Accuracy remains a **HYPOTHESIS**. Repeated Owner calls after checking Stayora are a qualitative failure signal.

## Network Adoption

Active Host, Active Sale, Active Butler, BQL operational usage, voluntary Sale/Host retention and measurable 30/60/90-day retention. Existing targets remain hypotheses.

## Operations

Arrival Coverage, QR/scoped-access usage, Check-in Capture Rate, Checkout Capture Rate, operational data completeness and operational exception rate. BQL using Stayora before falling back to Zalo/manual coordination is a qualitative signal.

## Commerce

Search → Property View → Request → Host Accept → Required Payment Condition → Booking Confirmed → Stay. Measure request acceptance, Request-to-Confirmed conversion, payment completion, cancellation/default, completed stays, Stayora-originated booking share, Booking/GMV where appropriate, CBV and Stayora Revenue. Booking share is distinct from Destination Stay Coverage.

## Trust and quality

Verified Supply Coverage, Verified Stays, Review Eligibility, Review Completion Rate, Incidents per Stay, incident resolution time and Verification reassessment/suspension/removal. Existing 20–30% Verified supply and ≥40% eligible completed stays producing reviews remain hypotheses.

## Guardrails

Double bookings caused by Stayora; unauthorized Inventory or Booking actions; incorrect Guest access; payment reconciliation discrepancy; privacy/data exposure; missing audit history; and manual overrides without provenance. Coverage must not be increased by sacrificing Inventory Integrity.
