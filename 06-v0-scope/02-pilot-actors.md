# Pilot Actors

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 5 DOCUMENTED; NOT FOUNDER FREEZE**

Operationally essential actors are Host/Owner, Sale, Butler, BQL/Destination Staff and Guest. Stayora Admin is supporting operational staff, not a PMF actor. Co-host remains an authority relationship/capability rather than a separate network to validate. Affiliate remains part of the long-term architecture but is not a V0 proof target.

Guest requests or participates in a Stay and can use scoped confirmation/access without a mandatory registered account. Stayora Admin may manually assist policy and exception work with attributable authority.
