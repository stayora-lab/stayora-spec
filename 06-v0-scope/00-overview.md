# V0 Overview

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 5 DOCUMENTED; NOT FOUNDER FREEZE**

V0 is the first Oceanami pilot for Stayora as an inventory-truth and destination-operations network with a real commerce path. It is not merely a small OTA MVP.

The pilot must let Owner, Sale, Butler, BQL/Destination Staff and Guest rely on one shared truth for availability and coordinate a stay from demand or external booking through arrival, stay and checkout. Stayora does not need to originate every booking to own useful Inventory Truth.

## V0 theses

1. Inventory Trust
2. Network Adoption
3. Destination Stay Coverage
4. Commerce Validation

A capability enters V0 only when it directly validates one of these theses or preserves operational/domain integrity. `MUST BUILD` means the integrity-sensitive capability is part of the pilot. `MANUAL-ASSISTED` means the domain truth exists while people execute complex policy. `DEFER / OUT OF V0` means it is explicitly outside the pilot boundary.

The four principles are: **Automate truth; manually assist complexity.** **Cut features, not integrity.** **Operational penetration → trusted inventory → network adoption → commerce growth.** Stayora does not need to own the booking to own inventory truth.
