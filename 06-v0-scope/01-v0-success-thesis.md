# V0 Success Thesis

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 5 DOCUMENTED; NOT FOUNDER FREEZE**

The pilot succeeds when evidence answers four questions:

- **Inventory Trust:** do users begin trusting Stayora as the availability source, without repeatedly calling the Owner to recheck?
- **Network Adoption:** do Host, Sale, Butler and BQL voluntarily return to the shared workflow?
- **Destination Stay Coverage:** does Stayora represent a meaningful share of actual stays, regardless of booking source?
- **Commerce Validation:** can Stayora originate and complete real bookings end to end?

These are evidence questions, not one weighted PASS/FAIL score. Existing numeric targets remain hypotheses unless explicitly labelled otherwise.
