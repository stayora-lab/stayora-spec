# Incident, Finding, Verification, Review and Reputation

> Status: **STABLE — CP7 ARCHITECTURE REVIEWED; NOT IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE**

## Incident and Finding

Incident is a Case/Aggregate candidate: `OPEN → ASSESSING → RESOLVED → CLOSED`. Complaint/Observation ≠ Incident ≠ Finding ≠ Responsibility ≠ Consequence. Operational resolution does not equal responsibility or economic/Verification consequence.

Finding is a first-class provenance concept when created. It may record subject, evidence basis, determination, responsible actor/component if determined, policy basis and time. Not every Incident creates a Finding; not every Finding creates punishment. Finding → Policy Evaluation → possible consequence.

## Verification

Keep separate Verification Assessment Case, Verification Review Case and Verification Status (`NOT_VERIFIED`, `VERIFIED`, `SUSPENDED`, `REMOVED`). Do not add `UNDER_REVIEW` to Status. Review Case may be OPEN while Status is VERIFIED. Serious-risk policy may cause precautionary suspension through explicit decision. Verification Decision preserves subject Property, originating Case, prior/resulting status, reason, standard/policy basis, authority and effective time. Never reduce Verification to a boolean.

## Review and Reputation

Review Right is an eligibility/entitlement record, separate from Review. Verified Interaction → Review Eligibility → Review Right → Review. Completed Stay is a normal evidence basis, not an unconditional right; external evidence may qualify. Review is a candidate Aggregate Root and may contain components for Property/Villa, Host, Butler, Sale or Destination where appropriate. Do not force a generic score across responsible components.

Reputation is a derived model from Reviews, Verified Transaction History, Findings, Commercial Reliability, Operational History and Platform Policy History. No universal `reputation_score` or TrustScore. Audience-specific projections may differ; internal signals are not automatically public. Reputation uses attributable observable evidence, not vague socioeconomic labels.
