# Conceptual Model Principles

> Status: **STABLE — CP7 ARCHITECTURE REVIEWED; NOT IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE**

## Identity and action

One person has one Stayora Identity. Guest, Host, Sale, Butler and Co-host are capacities/relationships, not separate account types. A critical action must be attributable to:

```text
Identity + Acting Capacity + Authority Basis + Resource + Action + Time
```

## Ownership and authority

Legal/economic Ownership ≠ Primary Host ≠ Commercial Authority ≠ Operational Assignment. Ownership explains whose asset it is; authority explains who may act; assignment explains who performs work; a commercial relationship explains how actors may transact. None silently implies the others.

## Canonical non-equivalences

`Identity ≠ Role`; `Ownership ≠ Authority`; `Relationship ≠ Authority`; `Request ≠ Booking`; `Request ≠ Inventory Commitment`; `Booking ≠ Stay`; `External Accommodation ≠ Stayora Booking`; `Inventory ≠ Availability`; `Payment ≠ Economic Entitlement`; `Settlement ≠ Payout`; `Incident ≠ Finding`; `Finding ≠ Responsibility`; `Finding ≠ Consequence`; `Review ≠ Reputation`; `Verification Review Case ≠ Verification Status`; `Cash Custody ≠ Revenue`; `Operational Participation ≠ Commercial Provenance`.

## Shared truth and projections

Acquisition and fulfillment may be separate. Marketplace, Sale, OTA and Owner Direct can converge into shared Inventory/Stay truth. Calendar, Availability, Search, Host Dashboard, Sale Earnings and Destination Today are projections; they are not alternate canonical business truth.

CP7 preserves provenance + temporal validity + immutable history. Corrections use new facts, adjustments, supersession or explicit correction actions. This does not require one giant event-sourcing architecture.
