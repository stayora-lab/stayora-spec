# Host Workspace

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 6 DOCUMENTED; NOT IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE**

```text
HOST
├── Home
├── Calendar
├── Requests
├── Bookings & Stays
├── Properties
├── Issues
├── Money
├── Quality
└── People & Access
```

Home is attention-first. Calendar is a portfolio-level operating view and visualizes the same effective Inventory Truth; it is not a second availability calculator. It may show Stayora commitments, external commitments, Temporary Commitments, Owner Blocks and maintenance/operational Blocks.

Requests remain separate from Bookings: a Request is not a pending Booking and does not reserve Inventory. Host/authorized actor accepts or rejects; conflicts are explicit. Booking and Stay can be connected in one work surface while preserving commercial versus operational lifecycles.

Calendar contextual actions may register External Booking/Stay, Owner Block or Maintenance Block. Capture operational/inventory truth required by the supported workflow, not external revenue, margin or payment details without an independent need. Do not create a fake Stayora Booking.

Properties covers listing, photos, amenities, capacity, location, Public Price, availability settings, Host/Co-host, Butler relationships and Stayora Verified. Money must keep Booking Value, Owner entitlement, Settlement and Payout distinct. Quality shows Verified and relevant cases/signals without a universal score. People & Access shows scoped relationships, not unrestricted commercial authority.
