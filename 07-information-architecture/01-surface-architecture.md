# Surface Architecture

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 6 DOCUMENTED; NOT IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE**

Stayora is one platform with contextual surfaces:

```text
STAYORA PLATFORM
├── PUBLIC MARKETPLACE
├── GUEST STAY ACCESS
├── PERSONAL [OPTIONAL / LATER]
├── HOST WORKSPACE
├── SALE WORKSPACE
├── OPERATIONS
│   ├── BUTLER CONTEXT
│   └── DESTINATION / BQL CONTEXT
└── STAYORA ADMIN
```

These are not independent products or separate business truths. They are projections over shared Identity, Inventory, Booking, Stay, Money, Verification and other domain truth. Same platform does not mean same workspace.

Public Marketplace serves discovery and conversion. Guest Stay Access serves a valid Stay. Host and Sale workspaces serve their responsibilities. Operations serves Butler and Destination/BQL work. Admin governs and resolves exceptions through explicit auditable actions.
