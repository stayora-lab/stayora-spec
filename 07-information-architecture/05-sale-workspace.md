# Sale Workspace

> Status: **STABLE — CHECKPOINT 6 DOCUMENTED; NOT IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE**

```text

```

Sale starts from customer demand: find availability → compare → quote/share → Guest agrees → Request → Host accepts → payment/confirmation → Stay → commission. Zalo may remain the Sale–Guest communication channel; Stayora owns structured inventory, request, confirmation, Stay and economic truth.

Find a Villa is the primary entry. Sale sees information needed to sell and fulfill, including real Availability, fit, Public Price and trust signals. Sale does not see Owner net payout, tax, profitability, full Owner ledger or unrelated internal economics. Direct Guest and Sale start from the same Public Price; Sale-funded discount does not silently reduce Owner entitlement.

A Request requires acceptance by the Host or another actor holding valid delegated Booking Authority. Sale capacity alone does not grant authority to accept the Request. A person who is also a Sale may accept only when acting under a separate valid delegated authority, such as an authorized Co-host capacity; acting capacity and authority basis remain attributable and auditable. Whitelist only unlocks Instant Book when property policy, platform eligibility and transaction conditions also pass. Instant Book is optional/controlled pilot. Lead and Lead Assignment remain separate; manual/admin assignment is valid V0. Earnings preserve Pending → Earned → Paid and later adjustments/reversals. My Network represents scoped Sale ↔ Commercial Authority holder relationships, not Platform Eligibility.

The core Sale journey must be efficient on mobile web. A full CRM, ranking engine and automated distribution engine are outside V0.
